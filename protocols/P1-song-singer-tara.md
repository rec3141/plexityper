# P1 draft · Does an exchange role recur across stations with different dominant suppliers?

**Data-blind discussion sketch, pending review and grid’s decision.** The candidate cohort is the 129 size-matched Tara Oceans DNA/RNA pairs listed in Salazar et al., Table W6. A station is the replication unit. Pair IDs, size interval, depth layer, coordinates and collection time must resolve before inclusion. Keep all samples from a station in the same fold and weight stations equally. This spatial comparison tests recurrence across locations. Temporal replacement and metabolite transfer require separate evidence. [Study tables and profiles](https://ocean-microbiome.org/v2/)

**Pairs and endpoint.** Primary contrast pairs a guild capable of de novo cobalamin synthesis with a prokaryotic recipient lineage carrying MetH and a corrinoid uptake system, with no supported MetE alternative or complete corrin-ring pathway. Include bacterial and archaeal suppliers. Apply the domain-aware ring and lower-ligand rules in notes/p2-incompatible-pairs.md. Allow the Thaumarchaeota combination of oxygen-independent ring synthesis and BluB without bacterial CobT. Track pseudocobalamin producers and unresolved lower-ligand chemistry separately. In sunlit strata, a secondary model adds pseudocobalamin-producer abundance, predicted ring-auxotrophic remodeler abundance (repair, DMB synthesis and activation), and their interaction. Co-occurrence measures potential supply. Exploratory contrast pairs a chemically annotated siderophore biosynthesis guild with recipients carrying a cognate receptor supported by ligand-specific evidence. Generic TonB annotations are insufficient. Within each eligible recipient genus, predict log uptake-system RNA relative to a fixed panel of expressed single-copy housekeeping genes, with recipient DNA coverage as a covariate. Freeze the panel, coverage filters, gene rules and eligible recipient definition before accessing expression. For the primary endpoint require an annotated cobalamin-responsive repressive riboswitch upstream of recipient btuB. Predict lower normalized btuB RNA with greater guild supply within a recipient lineage. Other transporter architectures are exploratory.

**Assignment.** Use versioned Tara assemblies and a matching dereplicated marine MAG catalog. Link genes to MAGs by assembly, contig and coordinates. Where identifiers differ require a unique sequence placement with concordant gene boundaries. Competitively map matched DNA and RNA against the same reference. Credit species or genera only when reads distinguish the alternatives. Retain ambiguous and unbinned resource markers in an explicit unresolved pool. Scale guild DNA and recipient DNA coverage by the station-sample median depth of a frozen panel of universal prokaryotic single-copy markers. Exclude samples below the frozen marker coverage floor. Report the unresolved fraction per station. Define the dominant supplier as a genus contributing over half of total guild DNA signal including the unresolved pool, with pathway completeness checked within its MAGs. Otherwise leave identity unresolved.

**Replication gates.** Proposed feasibility floor per resource–recipient comparison is 20 eligible stations, including at least two different dominant supplier genera represented at five or more stations each. Apply the station and genus floors separately within each depth layer and exact filter-size interval. Within that stratum both genera must occur in at least one common basin. Select stations using metadata and DNA alone. Repeated depths and technical replicates cannot satisfy the floor. These are design choices pending calibration on synthetic data, not a power calculation.

**Prediction and decision.** Fit environment-only, environment-plus-total-guild and guild-plus-genus models with identical recipient, depth, basin, season, light and nutrient covariates. Fit preprocessing and regularization inside training folds. For both primary model comparisons, leave one station out at a time within each eligible stratum. Score dominant genera represented at four or more other training stations in every scored fold. For a separate transfer test, hold out each dominant genus in turn, removing all training stations where it dominates any sample. Compare only the guild and environment models in those folds. Report transfer to an unseen dominant genus only when its guild-gain lower bound exceeds the proposed margin. For each scheme aggregate paired differences in expected log predictive density in nats per station, with station-block bootstrap intervals. Proposed margin is 0.1 nat per station. Support role recurrence if the lower 95% bound for guild gain over environment exceeds the margin, the entire identity-gain interval lies within ±0.1, and the upper 95% station-bootstrap bound for the adjusted guild slope is below zero. A predictive gain with an opposite or unresolved sign leaves the proposed supply response inconclusive. Support an identity contribution if its lower bound exceeds 0.1. If the guild gain upper bound is below 0.1, report no meaningful guild gain under this model. Report other intervals, failed replication gates, or conclusions reversed by leaving a basin out as inconclusive. Report leave-one-basin-out estimates alongside the primary estimates and flag unavailable contrasts when geographic overlap fails. Keep the exploratory siderophore result separate from the primary decision.
