# What persists when the partners change?

**Astra’s proposal for the group manuscript.** Lead with a question that admits several biologically distinct answers. When an aquatic assemblage reappears, what recurs at the same time and place: the same populations, the same exchange roles, both, or neither? The case in which one recurs without the other offers a reason to measure a plexitype and a plexiome separately.

Goldford et al. give a concrete comparator. Replicate communities converged at coarse taxonomic resolution despite variation at finer resolution. In their four-isolate test, each isolate used byproducts of the other isolates and each could grow independently on the supplied glucose. An experimentally observed, densely connected network of facilitation can therefore have zero obligate partners for that growth endpoint. Read the original main-text results and Fig. 3. [Goldford et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405290/)

## Working vocabulary for the first paper

| Term | How I would use it | Observation that changes the interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Plexitype** | A recurring, potentially overlapping subset of taxa at a declared resolution and scale. On independent events, test whether its observed members help predict masked members beyond measured habitat, continuous background gradients and matched nonmembers. | If matched nonmembers predict equally well within a predeclared margin, the proposed membership lacks predictive specificity. Claim discrete whole-community states only after a separate comparison using identical inputs. |
| **Plexon** | One occurrence with an independent boundary such as an isolated particle, identified host individual, or tracked parcel. Physical boundaries define where to test membership. | Pooled filters and fixed-station snapshots can leave the number of occurrences unresolved. Omit the term from results without independent boundaries. |
| **Plexibiont** | A hypothesis that a specified assemblage’s persistence or activity depends on interactions among its members under stated conditions. Keep host association as an observed property. Demonstrating that the same assemblage can function with or without a host would be a separate finding. | A group can recur because all its members occupy the same niche. Condition-specific removal or natural replacement with independent functional evidence tests dependence. |
| **Plexiome** | A condition-specific account of exchanges, naming the donor, resource, recipient, direction, and evidence for each. Keep unknown donors and environmental pools explicit. | A switch in dominant donor can preserve a resource transfer role while changing the taxon-level network. Compare both representations. |
| **Plexitrophy** | A falsifiable claim about the minimum set of distinct organisms sufficient for a named nutritional endpoint under specified external supplies. Say explicitly if the count includes the focal organism. Track interchangeable donors. | One focal organism needing two distinct donor roles makes a minimum of three organisms including the focal member. A pair, substitute, unbinned producer, or ambient resource can change the bound. Genome-based results are conditional model predictions. |

**Architecture.** Preserve the historical meaning of holobiont as a host and its associated microbes. A host-associated community can contain lateral exchanges among symbionts, while a free-living group can have a microbial hub. Record host association and graph shape on separate axes. Use ordinary “star,” “path,” “mesh” and “modular” until a biological prediction survives uncertainty in the edges. Keep dictyobiont and catenabiont in the glossary pending that test. In an undirected three-node graph a path is also a star. Even with directed edges, a fork and an unconfounded chain sharing the same middle node entail the same conditional independence between the ends. A physical host can anchor a sample boundary but cannot orient every metabolite edge. Read the original conceptual essay for host terminology. [Bordenstein and Theis](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002226)

**Novelty.** Recurrent plankton associations, community types, cross-feeding and exchange modeling already have names. The opportunity is a reusable experiment that separates recurrence of composition, recurrence of exchange roles, and continuity of an occurrence. Freeze resolution before inspection. Record how long the type was observed, where investigators looked for it and which collections themselves have defensible boundaries. The prototype paper needs only the term corresponding to its strongest result. [Prior art and evidence for sampling boundaries](plexitype-recurrence.md)

## Three hypotheses for the local collections

1. **Composition predicts missing members in a new year.** Within a site, mask prespecified members on held-out whole years or events. Compare a model with other members against models of season, measured environment, continuous background community gradients and matched nonmembers. Estimate predictive gain per independent event and preregister a useful gain. A precise gain below that value weakens the claim. Inadequate overlap or wide uncertainty leaves it unresolved. Run on an existing microscape ASV count table at a consistent primer region, then test a fixed resolution in independently collected shotgun data where available. A stable group requiring no residual predictive gain remains a recognizable seasonal association. [Detailed design](plexitype-recurrence.md)
2. **An exchange role predicts recipient activity despite donor replacement.** Find matched independent environmental events with comparable aggregate donor capacity, DNA abundance and recipient coverage, but different dominant donor lineages. Use existing danaSeq per-MAG KO/module tables and paired RNA counts. Compare prediction of a prespecified recipient readout from donor identity versus exchange-compatible donor roles; exclude the recipient from the supply predictor. Include covariates for DNA copy abundance and library size and test against matched non-exchange functions. Evaluate whole-event holdouts. If donor roles transfer and identities vary, prioritize function-level recurrence. If identity reproducibly adds prediction, prioritize partner specificity. Environmental supply, common pulses and unmeasured donors can explain either pattern. Absent a dissolved-metabolite measurement, report potential supply. Equivalence needs a sufficiently precise interval around a prespecified tolerance.
3. **The bulk signature resides together at the proposed scale.** Choose an existing survey only if it includes replicated, individually resolved patches or host IDs and comparable controls from the same event. Apply a frozen compositional rule to each patch and compare joint occupancy with what prevalence, patch size and sampling effort predict. If the members consistently segregate among adequately observed patches, their coherent bulk signature fails as a single-patch plexon. For a purported parcel-scale plexon substitute physically tracked inside/outside parcel samples across time. Size-fraction data alone support a contrast between fractions and leave individuality open. [Primary field comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10811735/)
