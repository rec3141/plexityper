## 1. Mathematical & Statistical Issues Identified Prior to Event-Outcome Inspection

1. **Nested 3-state fixed-margin fiber connectivity vs. exact allowed-set enumeration (`249726888184299520`, `249764283436941312`, `249779947258167296`):** Frozen `P2` (`e0dd8645dd32`, §3.1 & §3.4) evaluates candidate auxotrophy across a monotone completion pair $(I^-, I^+)$ satisfying cellwise nesting $\text{definite} \subseteq \text{possible}$ ($M^-_{i,j} \le M^+_{i,j}$), encoded as a ternary matrix $S_{i,j} = M^-_{i,j} + M^+_{i,j} \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ (`0 = ABSENT`, `1 = UNRESOLVED`, `2 = PRESENT`). General 3-state fixed-margin fibers and row-constrained fibers do **not** admit a bounded-size local Markov basis: under the symmetric local kernel $K = \frac{1}{2} K_{\text{row}} + \frac{1}{2} K_{\text{col}}$, the reachable component $\mathcal{C}_K(S_{\text{obs}})$ may be a strict subset of the full allowed set $\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})$. Crucially:
   - When $\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})$ is small enough for **exact backtracking enumeration** ($|\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})| \le 2{,}000$), enumerating the **entire allowed set $\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})$** yields the exact **full-fiber Null 2 mean** directly, **even when local moves $K$ are disconnected** ($|\mathcal{C}_K(S_{\text{obs}})| < |\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})|$).
   - When $|\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})| > 2{,}000$ so MCMC under $K$ is required, four chains started in $\mathcal{C}_K(S_{\text{obs}})$ cannot escape $\mathcal{C}_K(S_{\text{obs}})$ and can pass empirical $\hat{R}$ and $\text{ESS}$ checks within one component while missing other components entirely. Therefore, **local-move connectivity must remain a separate gate** from empirical MCMC diagnostics whenever full-fiber enumeration is infeasible.
2. **Besag–Clifford rank exchangeability vs. uniform stationary-mean estimation (`249778991556640768`):** Besag & Clifford (1989, *Biometrika* 76:633–642) proves finite-sample rank exchangeability of $(T(S_{\text{obs}}), T(S^{(1)}), \dots, T(S^{(D)}))$ conditional on a single hub $H \sim K^L(S_{\text{obs}}, \cdot)$ for **Monte Carlo rank p-values**; it does **not** make the finite-$L$ spoke distribution $K^L(H, \cdot)$ equal to the uniform stationary distribution $\text{Unif}(\Omega)$ or certify a prevalence-excess confidence interval (e.g., on $\{0, 1\}$ with switch probability $0.01$ and $L = 20$, hub $H = 0$ yields spoke mean $(1 - 0.98^{20})/2 \approx 0.1662$ vs. uniform mean $0.50$). Spoke counts around a finite-$L$ hub are therefore **never** pooled into prevalence-excess or log-OR bounds.
3. **Observation-invariant target fiber for constrained Null 2c (`249778991556640768`):** Exempting observed clash rows (`exempt_rows`) while still permitting them to trade allows a clashing row to lose its clash during sampling, reaching states $Y$ that would define a different exempt set ($\Omega(Y) \neq \Omega(S_{\text{obs}})$). Freezing observed clash rows completely (`locked_rows = R_clash(S_obs)`) while enforcing strict $\text{O}_2^+ \times \text{O}_2^-$ exclusion with **zero exemptions** on all remaining rows defines a true equivalence-class fiber $[S_{\text{obs}}]_{\sim_{2c}}$ invariant to which state in the fiber was observed.
4. **Governance principle (`249765089133379584`, `249765136390602752`):** Conditioning Null 2 on $\mathcal{C}_K(S_{\text{obs}})$ when full-fiber $\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})$ is neither enumerated nor certified connected changes the reference population relative to frozen `P2` (`e0dd8645dd32`) and remains **held from primary use** (`--p2-a1-approved` defaults to `False`) pending cross-family review and `grid`'s explicit approval.

## 2. Move Connectivity, Two-Tier Stationary-Mean Estimation, and Null 2c Target

### 2.1 Three-State Move-Set Connectivity Recorded Per Event
For every evaluated event matrix $S_{\text{obs}} \in \{0, 1, 2\}^{N \times M}$, local move-set connectivity under $K = \frac{1}{2} K_{\text{row}} + \frac{1}{2} K_{\text{col}}$ is classified into **three** mutually exclusive states (`249765356272795648`, `249765396898824192`):
1. **`connected`:** Certified either by exact enumeration + BFS proving $|\mathcal{C}_K(S_{\text{obs}})| = |\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})|$ ($|\Omega| \le 2{,}000$), or (on unconstrained Null 2b with zero locked/forbidden cells or row predicates when $|\Omega| > 2{,}000$) by Ryser's Theorem (1957) when at most two symbols in $\{0, 1, 2\}$ have positive remaining count on the active non-constant submatrix.
2. **`disconnected (proven)`:** Exact enumeration + BFS proves $|\mathcal{C}_K(S_{\text{obs}})| < |\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})|$ (recording exact `component_size` and `fiber_size`). The label `disconnected` is used **only** when disconnection is proven.
3. **`connectivity unknown`:** $|\Omega(S_{\text{obs}})|$ (or backtracking search) exceeds the exact enumeration budget (`2,000` states / `50,000` search nodes) and no theorem certifies connectivity.

### 2.2 Two-Tier Stationary-Mean Estimation & Separate Connectivity / Mixing Gates
For each stratum or contrast in Event $e$ (with fixed eligible recipient denominator $N_{e,\text{obs}} \ge 1$), let $p_{\text{low}}(S) = N_{\text{plexi}}(S) / N_{e,\text{obs}} \in [0, 1]$ and $p_{\text{high}}(S) = (N_{\text{plexi}}(S) + U(S)) / N_{e,\text{obs}} \in [0, 1]$ be the **exact prevalence bounds used in the verdict**:

1. **Tier 1 — Exact Uniform Allowed-Set Enumeration ($|\Omega(S_{e,\text{obs}})| \le 2{,}000$):**
   - **Full-fiber mean (`full_fiber_available = True`, zero MC error — even if `move_connectivity == 'disconnected (proven)'`!):** Score **every** state $S \in \Omega(S_{e,\text{obs}})$ with uniform weight $1 / |\Omega(S_{e,\text{obs}})|$ to obtain exact uniform full-fiber means $(\mu^{\Omega}_{e,\text{low}}, \mu^{\Omega}_{e,\text{high}})$ with $\text{MCSE} = 0$.
   - **Component-conditional mean (`P2-A1` sensitivity when `disconnected (proven)`):** Separately average over $S \in \mathcal{C}_K(S_{e,\text{obs}})$ with uniform weight $1 / |\mathcal{C}_K(S_{e,\text{obs}})|$ to obtain $(\mu^{\mathcal{C}}_{e,\text{low}}, \mu^{\mathcal{C}}_{e,\text{high}})$.
2. **Tier 2 — Pre-Specified 4-Chain Reversible MCMC Stationary-Mean Estimator ($|\Omega(S_{e,\text{obs}})| > 2{,}000$):**
   - Run $M = 4$ independent reversible chains under symmetric $K = \frac{1}{2} K_{\text{row}} + \frac{1}{2} K_{\text{col}}$ (proposing uniformly over the fixed-margin non-constant free rows and non-constant columns) from $S_{e,\text{obs}}$, each with burn-in $B = \max(40,\; 20\max(N_e, M_e))$ steps followed by $N_c = D / 4$ recorded draws ($250$/chain at protocol $D = 1{,}000$) thinned by $\tau = \max(12,\; 3\max(N_e, M_e))$ steps per draw.
   - **Diagnostics on the prevalence bounds used in the verdict (`mcmc_diagnostics_pass`):** Compute Gelman–Rubin split-$\hat{R}$ ($2M = 8$ half-chains) and Geyer positive-pair bulk $\text{ESS}$ **directly on the per-draw prevalence-bound series $p^{(m,t)}_{\text{low}}$ and $p^{(m,t)}_{\text{high}}$** (for each non-constant bound series), alongside active-cell movement $\|S - S_{e,\text{obs}}\|_1$. Require:
     1. Non-zero state movement across chains whenever at least one active 2-line move with $>1$ completion exists, and
     2. **`split_Rhat <= 1.05` AND `ESS >= 200`** (`max(40, 0.20 * D)` in fast non-inferential self-tests) on every non-constant prevalence-bound series ($p_{\text{low}}, p_{\text{high}}$).
     *(Explicit caveat per `249779947258167296`: empirical $\hat{R}$ and $\text{ESS}$ checks are necessary convergence diagnostics, **not** a general mathematical guarantee of finite-time mixing or multi-component exploration.)*
   - **Separate connectivity gate on Tier 2:** A Tier 2 event yields a valid primary full-fiber estimate **if and only if** BOTH (a) `move_connectivity == 'connected'` **and** (b) `mcmc_diagnostics_pass == True`. If `move_connectivity != 'connected'`, Tier 2 chains are confined to $\mathcal{C}_K(S_{e,\text{obs}})$ and **cannot** certify the primary full-fiber Null 2 regardless of $\hat{R}$ or $\text{ESS}$.
   - **20-batch MCSE outer widening inside event bootstrap:** Partition the $4$ chains into $K_{\text{batch}} = 20$ non-overlapping batches ($5$/chain) on $p_{\text{low}}$ and $p_{\text{high}}$ to obtain batch-means variances $\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{BM}}(\hat{p}_{e,\text{low}})$ and $\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{BM}}(\hat{p}_{e,\text{high}})$. In each event-cluster bootstrap resample (where event $e$ has multiplicity $m_e$ and weight $w_e = m_e N_{e,\text{obs}} / \sum_j m_j N_{j,\text{obs}}$), widen the pooled null interval $[c, d]$ outward by $\pm t_{19, 0.995}\,\text{MCSE}_{\text{pool}}$ ($t_{19, 0.995} = 2.861$, clamped to $[0, 1]$):
     $$c_{\text{outer}} = \max\!\left(0,\; c - 2.861\sqrt{\sum_e w_e^2\,\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{BM}}(\hat{p}_{e,\text{low}})}\right), \qquad d_{\text{outer}} = \min\!\left(1,\; d + 2.861\sqrt{\sum_e w_e^2\,\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{BM}}(\hat{p}_{e,\text{high}})}\right).$$
   - **Mandatory unit test cases (`--self-test`):**
     1. *Two-component unequal-mean fixture (`249779947258167296`):* A `disconnected (proven)` fiber where Component 1 and Component 2 have distinct `R-I3` plexitrophy means (`1.0` vs. `0.0`), verifying that Tier 1 exact enumeration of $\Omega$ recovers the exact full-null mean (`0.50`) while component-conditional enumeration on $\mathcal{C}_K(S_{\text{obs}})$ reports `1.00`.
     2. *Known-fiber Tier 2 calibration test:* Verify on nontrivial enumerated fibers that Tier 2 passes `mcmc_diagnostics_pass` and achieves $\ge 90\%$ multi-seed coverage of $\mu^{\Omega}_{\text{exact}}$.
     3. *Slow-kernel regression test case (`p_switch = 0.01, L = 20`):* Regression test verifying that spoke mean from hub $0$ reproduces $(1 - 0.98^{20})/2 \approx 0.1662 \neq 0.50$ and that `mcmc_diagnostics_pass` rejects the under-mixed chain (treated strictly as a regression test case, not a general mixing proof).
     4. *Mixed Tier 1 + Tier 2 cohort per-event source selection test (`249788802780282880`):* Equal-weight mixed cohort combining a disconnected Tier 1 event (`full = 0.50`, `component = 1.00`) with a non-connected Tier 2 event (`component = 0.00`, `mcmc_diagnostics_pass = True`), verifying that without approval the mixed primary is `unavailable` (`None`), whereas under Option A (`--p2-a1-approved`) per-event selection retains Event 1's exact full mean (`0.50`) and falls back to Event 2's component mean (`0.00`) to pool to **`0.25`** for primary while keeping the separate all-component sensitivity at **`0.50`**.

### 2.3 Observation-Invariant Equivalence-Class Target for Constrained Null 2c
Let $R_{\text{clash}}(S) = \{r \in \{0, \dots, N-1\} : \{c : S_{r,c} = 2\} \cap \mathcal{O}_+ \neq \emptyset \;\land\; \{c : S_{r,c} = 2\} \cap \mathcal{O}_- \neq \emptyset\}$. Null 2c restricts permutations to the equivalence class $[S_{\text{obs}}]_{\sim_{2c}}$:
$$S \sim_{2c} S' \iff R_{\text{clash}}(S) = R_{\text{clash}}(S'), \quad S_r = S'_r \;\; (\forall r \in R_{\text{clash}}(S)), \quad \text{and identical ternary row \& column margins}.$$
All cells in rows $r \in R_{\text{clash}}(S_{\text{obs}})$ are locked (`locked_rows`) across both row and column moves, while strict $\mathcal{O}_+ \times \mathcal{O}_-$ exclusion is enforced with **zero exemptions** on all $r \notin R_{\text{clash}}(S_{\text{obs}})$, so $[S']_{\sim_{2c}} \equiv [S_{\text{obs}}]_{\sim_{2c}}$ for every $S' \in [S_{\text{obs}}]_{\sim_{2c}}$.

## 3. Binding Pre-Approval Reporting & Symmetric Partial-Information Rules (`--p2-a1-approved` = `False` Default)

### 3.1 P2-Q1 (Symmetric Frozen §4.3 Partial-Information Rule Over Null 1 and Primary Null 2b)
After Data-Adequacy Gates 1–3 and the `1,000`-draw requirement pass, define:
- `null1_valid`: `True` iff `hits_by_mag` is present, every eligible event has $\ge 1$ detected $\ge 95\%$-complete source MAG with gene-hit blocks, and $\ge 10$ (`min_events`) eligible events yield $\ge 1$ simulated candidate auxotroph across `1,000` draws.
- `null2b_primary_valid`: Selected **per event** (`primary_null2_source` recorded per event in `null2b_per_event_connectivity`; `249788802780282880`):
  - If Event $e$ has a certified full-fiber stationary mean — i.e., **Tier 1 exact allowed-set enumeration** (`tier1_exact_full_fiber`, $|\Omega_e| \le 2{,}000$, valid regardless of local-move connectivity!) **or** **Tier 2 with BOTH `move_connectivity == 'connected'` AND `mcmc_diagnostics_pass == True`** (`tier2_connected_full_fiber`, with 20-batch MCSE outer widening) — Event $e$ **always** contributes its full-fiber stationary mean to primary Null 2b (even when `--p2-a1-approved` is `True`).
  - Else if `--p2-a1-approved` is `True` (Option A) AND Event $e$ is an unenumerated non-connected Tier 2 event with `mcmc_diagnostics_pass == True`, Event $e$ contributes its MCSE-widened component-conditional $\mathcal{C}_K(S_{e,\text{obs}})$ estimate (`component_conditional_p2_a1`).
  - Else Event $e$'s `primary_null2_source` is `unavailable`. Cohort `null2b_primary_valid` is `True` iff **every** eligible event has `primary_null2_source != 'unavailable'`. *(While `--p2-a1-approved` is `False`, unenumerated non-connected Tier 2 events are **never** treated as valid for either `Supported` or OR-`Falsified`.)*

Then:
1. **Both `null1_valid` and `null2b_primary_valid`:** Pass both $[L_{p,1}^{\text{id}}, U_{p,1}^{\text{id}}]$ and $[L_{p,2}^{\text{id}}, U_{p,2}^{\text{id}}]$ to frozen `decide_p2_q1`.
2. **`null1_valid` only (`null2b_primary_valid == False`, occurring on Tier 2 events where $|\Omega_e| > 2{,}000$):**
   - If $U_{p,1}^{\text{id}} < +0.05$: emit **`Falsified`** (valid Null 1 upper bound $< +0.05$ suffices under §4.3).
   - Else if $L_{p,1}^{\text{id}} \le +0.05$: emit **`Inconclusive`**.
   - Else ($L_{p,1}^{\text{id}} > +0.05$): emit:
     - **`Inconclusive (primary Null 2 unavailable: move set disconnected)`** when unenumerated Tier 2 cohort connectivity is `disconnected (proven)`,
     - **`Inconclusive (primary Null 2 unavailable: connectivity unknown)`** when Tier 2 cohort connectivity is `connectivity unknown`, or
     - **`Inconclusive (primary Null 2 unavailable: MCMC stationary-mean mixing unvalidated)`** when Tier 2 cohort connectivity is `connected` but `mcmc_diagnostics_pass` failed on $(p_{\text{low}}, p_{\text{high}})$.
3. **`null2b_primary_valid` only (`null1_valid == False`, symmetric OR-falsification per `249778991556640768`):**
   - If $U_{p,2}^{\text{id}} < +0.05$: emit **`Falsified`** (an independently valid, certified full-fiber primary Null 2b upper bound $< +0.05$ suffices under §4.3).
   - Else ($U_{p,2}^{\text{id}} \ge +0.05$): emit **`Inconclusive (Null 1 unevaluable: <reason>)`**.
4. **Neither `null1_valid` nor `null2b_primary_valid`:** Emit **`Inconclusive (Null 1 unevaluable: <reason>; primary Null 2 unavailable: <reason>)`**.
5. **Separate all-component sensitivity field:** Whenever any eligible event has `move_connectivity != 'connected'`, the all-component $\mathcal{C}_K(S_{e,\text{obs}})$ interval (pooling $\mathcal{C}_K$ across events from Tier 1 exact BFS component enumeration or Tier 2 MCMC with `mcmc_diagnostics_pass`) is emitted strictly in its own separate sensitivity field labelled **`component-conditional (P2-A1, pending grid)`** (or `grid-approved`), never overwriting available exact full-fiber means in the per-event primary selection.

### 3.2 P2-Q2 (Matched Incompatible vs. Compatible Recipient Pairs)
1. **Primary P2-Q2 verdict is NOT blocked by missing full-fiber Null 2c (`249765356272795648`, `249765396898824192`):** Decided strictly from the `1,000`-draw event-cluster bootstrap Manski log-odds interval $[L_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}}, U_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}}]$ on outcome-blind 1-to-1 GTDB-clade + covariate-matched pairs against $\ln 2$ per supply scenario $E$ after Gates 1–3, alongside `u_inc_over_n_inc`, `u_comp_over_n_comp`, and `resolved_only_ci_log_or`.
2. **Null 2c attribution:** Evaluated on $[S_{\text{obs}}]_{\sim_{2c}}$ (§2.3) using the **same per-event primary source selection** (`primary_null2c_source` recorded per event: exact $\Omega$ on Tier 1 or connected+MCMC-validated Tier 2, falling back to $\mathcal{C}_K$ under Option A **only** on individual unenumerated non-connected Tier 2 events), while keeping the all-component $\mathcal{C}_K$ interval separate in `component_conditional_sensitivity`.

## 4. Decision Requested From `grid` (Only After Cross-Family Sign-Off and Prior to Examining Event Outcomes)
