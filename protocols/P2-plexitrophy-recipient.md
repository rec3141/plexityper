## 1. Question, Formal P-all Definition, and Epistemic Scope

- **Plain-language gloss & graph invariants (`249243505658347520`–`249243668720304128`, `249266374643728384`, `249266530776694784`, `249267376574873600`):** For any capability completion $I$, let $\mathcal{W}^{(I)}(X, E)$ denote the set of **all feasible $S_1$ partner wirings** $(S, E_S)$ for focal organism $X$ under environmental supply $E$ (with no restriction to inclusion-minimal organism sets). At completion $I$, `decide_nonplexi_p_all` returns `plexi` iff $\mathcal{W}^{(I)}(X, E) \neq \emptyset$ **and in every feasible $S_1$ wiring** $(S, E_S) \in \mathcal{W}^{(I)}(X, E)$, $X$ either draws directly from **at least two immediate living suppliers** ($d_{S,E_S}(X) \ge 2$) or **itself lies on a simple directed exchange cycle of length $\ge 3$** ($\kappa_{S,E_S}(X) = 1$); when $\mathcal{W}^{(I)}(X, E) = \emptyset$, `decide_nonplexi_p_all` returns `no_donor` (never vacuously `plexi`). Across the conservative/optimistic pair $(I^-, I^+)$ (`p2_scorer.py:1500–1508`), resolved $I^-$ calls take precedence (`d0` $\mapsto$ `d0_ambient`, `not_plexi` $\mapsto$ `d1_or_chain`); after the resolved $I^-$ calls are handled, a tuple is called `unresolved_no_donor` iff **even $I^+$ has no feasible wiring** ($\mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}(X, E) = \emptyset$), `plexitrophic` iff **both** $I^-$ and $I^+$ return `plexi`, and `unresolved_ambig_donor` when $\mathcal{W}^{(I^-)}(X, E) = \emptyset$ while $\mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}(X, E) \neq \emptyset$ (or, after the resolved $I^-$ calls are handled, when $I^-$ and $I^+$ otherwise disagree). Consortium size $m_{S,E_S}(X) = |S|$ (and descriptive upstream supplier count $d_{\text{lb,up}}(X)$ and SCC size $c_{S,E_S}(X) = |\text{SCC}_{S,E_S}(X)|$) are reported in prose alongside $(d, \kappa)$ without introducing extra coinages. Quantifying over **all** feasible $S_1$ wirings $\mathcal{W}^{(I)}(X, E)$ rather than inclusion-minimal sets is essential (`249266374643728384`): if `A` produces `{a, p}`, `B` produces `{b, q}`, `C` converts `p + q -> {a, b}`, and `X` needs `{a, b}`, then `{X, A, B}` is the unique inclusion-minimal viable set ($d_X = 2$), yet the larger feasible wiring `{X, A, B, C}` (`A -> C`, `B -> C`, `C -> X`) allows `C` alone to supply `X` ($d_X = 1, \kappa_X = 0 \implies$ `not plexitrophic`). Neither pure serial chains (`D1 -> D2 -> X`: $d_X = 1, m_X = 3, \kappa_X = 0$) nor chained mutualistic dyads where $X$ sits at an end (`X <-> A <-> B`: $d_X = 1, m_X = 3, c_X = 3, \kappa_X = 0$) qualify as plexitrophic.
- **Genomic inference vs. experimental essentiality (`249265185466269696`):** P2 scores **genome-inferred candidate auxotrophy** and **genome-inferred candidate plexitrophy** from MAG gene-content networks and co-detection. Throughout this protocol and any manuscript reporting, a genome-based `plexitrophic` call denotes **candidate genomic irreducibility across detected co-occurring genomes**, kept strictly distinct from an **experimentally established obligate nutritional requirement** (which requires physiological/co-culture demonstration).
- **Strict single-node grounded Horn semantics $S_1$ (`discussion/deep/plexitrophy-computation.md` `c7e4b04a800d`):** A support + transfer wiring $(S, E_S) \in \mathcal{W}^{(I)}(X, E)$ is feasible only if every member of $S$ is backward-reachable from $X$ and there exists a single acyclic proof DAG over `(organism, metabolite)` nodes in which each `(u, m)` has at most one justification and every exported metabolite $(u \to v : m)$ is **rule-produced** by $u$ (never imported and relayed by $u$, blocking 1-metabolite $p \leftrightarrow q$, 2-metabolite $p_1 \leftrightarrow p_2$, and cross-organism `D:p -> D:q -> B:r -> D:p` laundering).

1. **P2-Q1 (Primary estimand — recipient-level one-level fan-in beyond artifact & guild-stacking nulls):** The **primary P2 estimand is one-level fan-in** ($d_{S,E_S}(X) \ge 2$, where co-occurring donors carry no modeled needs so $\kappa_{S,E_S}(X) = 0$ identically by construction in `mode='fanin_only'`).    exceed both (a) a **recipient + donor contig-block fragmentation downsampling null (Null 1)** and (b) a **genome-size- and prevalence-preserving pathway-permutation ("Shooting-the-Moon" stacking) null (Null 2b)** by at least a pre-registered margin?
   - *Primary solver (`mode='fanin_only'` in `scratch/p2_scorer.py` `579e9c3502c1`):* Evaluates $\mathcal{W}^{(I)}_{\text{fanin}}(X, E)$ separately at $I^-$ and $I^+$ over **all feasible $S_1$ wirings of arbitrary size** in exact $O(|V|)$ polynomial time via `two_node_smax` (Theorem 3 + Lemma L5; 100% of P2 pathway rules satisfy `is_metabolite_dep_acyclic`, requiring zero bundle or support enumeration; $\mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}_{\text{fanin}}(X, E) = \emptyset \mapsto \texttt{unresolved\_no\_donor}$, while $\mathcal{W}^{(I^-)}_{\text{fanin}}(X, E) = \emptyset \neq \mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}_{\text{fanin}}(X, E) \mapsto \texttt{unresolved\_ambig\_donor}$, `p2_scorer.py:1504–1508`).

## 3. Method

### 3.1 Non-Redundant Genome Set, Two-Point Monotone Completion $(I^-, I^+)$, and Eukaryotic Donor Class
1. **Two-step deduplication & focal prokaryotic scope (`filter_derep_prokaryotic_mags`):**
   - *Focal recipient scope:* Focal recipients $X$ are strictly **prokaryotic (`Bacteria` and `Archaea`)** because CheckM2 completeness, intronless operon `p_intact`, and Bakta CDS models do not calibrate recipient false-absence probabilities on spliced eukaryotes (`248926030043463680`).
   - *Step 1a (Consensus binner filter):* Restrict prokaryotic bins within each assembly strictly to **one consensus bin set (`dastool-*`)**, discarding raw component binners.
   - *Step 1b (Species-level ANI dereplication):* Dereplicate the `dastool-*` bins across the cohort at **95% ANI (`skani` / `galah`)** and report both pre-derep consensus counts and post-95%-ANI dereplicated counts separately.
2. **Per-event community $\mathcal{G}_e$ & calibrated recipient eligibility:**
   - Block samples into independent field events (cruise/campaign $\times$ station; same-cast depths and technical/sequencing replicates grouped). A dereplicated MAG is **detected** in event $e$ at primary breadth $\ge 50\%$ (sensitivity check: $\ge 70\%$ breadth and equalized prokaryotic-mapped read depth).
   - *Focal recipient candidate-auxotroph eligibility (frozen once from $X$'s genome alone before donor or $E$ inspection):* Prokaryotic (`Bacteria`/`Archaea`), detected in event $e$, CheckM2 completeness $\ge 90\%$, contamination $\le 5\%$, and pathway state `absent` with **calibrated false-absence bound $p_{\text{false\_absence}} < 0.05$** (`estimate_false_absence_prob`, including single-gene `metE` `K00549` detectability in `evaluate_recipient_b12_auxotrophy`; uncalibrated or overly fragmented MAGs return `not_eligible` even at zero KO hits, per `248968746164994048 #4`). Genome-autonomous MAGs (`present` in $X$) are counted separately in $N_{\text{auto}}$ and never enter $N_{\text{cand\_aux}}$.
3. **Two-point monotone completion $(I^-, I^+)$ across prokaryotic donors, eukaryotic donors, and unbinned pools (`evaluate_recipient_requirements_in_event`):**
   - *Prokaryotic MAG donors:* Under conservative completion $I^-$, a donor exports only routes with `STATE_PRESENT` (and in `consortium_cyc` requires any endpoint in `STATE_ABSENT` or `STATE_UNRESOLVED`). Under optimistic completion $I^+$, a donor exports routes in `STATE_PRESENT` or `STATE_UNRESOLVED` (and requires only calibrated `STATE_ABSENT` endpoints).
   - *Organism-resolved eukaryotic donors (`eukaryotic_donors`, per `248976762356547584`, `248976870271795200`, `248978604545196032`):* In $I^-$, export only routes with `STATE_PRESENT` in that single eukaryotic genome. In $I^+$ (default `euk_annotated_routes_only=False`), biologically excluded prokaryote-only pathways (`P_B12_AER`, `A_B12_ANA`, `P_BLUB*`, `A_BZA`, `A_NIF`, `A_DSR`, `A_MCR`, `A_WL`, `P_CU_MMO`, `P_THIO_AER`) evaluate to `STATE_ABSENT` ($p_{\text{fa}} = 0$), whereas eukaryote-eligible pathways (`THI` including `THI4` `K03146` / `TPK1` `K00949`, `C0` amino acids/cofactors, `B12_LOWER_ATTACH`, `ASSIM_SO4`, `ASSIM_NO3`) with uncalibrated eukaryotic assembly/splicing detectability evaluate to `STATE_UNRESOLVED` ($p_{\text{fa}} = 1.0 \ge 0.05$) and are **retained in $I^+$**, conservatively downgrading putative prokaryote-only fan-in to `unresolved_ambig_donor` unless eukaryotic absence detectability is explicitly calibrated (`false_absence_probs < 0.05`). Report the matched-event transition matrix (`compare_calls_with_eukaryotic_donors`) and the conditional `euk_annotated_routes_only=True` bound alongside.
   - *Fungal denitrification note (`248979843727147008`):* `A_NOS_NOR` (`norBC` `K04561+K02305` / `nosZ` `K00376`) is currently descriptive (`I4`, not an auxotrophic P2 recipient endpoint) and restricted to `PROKARYOTIC_DOMAINS`; if any future extension evaluates `NO -> N2O` donor capacity across eukaryotes, fungal cytochrome P450 nitric-oxide reductase `P450nor` (`K15877`, `[EC:1.7.1.14]`, verified in `ko_list`) must be added via `DomainSwitch` rather than treated as biologically absent in `Fungi`.
   - *Unbinned prokaryotic contigs (`unbinned_genome`) & pooled unassigned eukaryotic hits (`unassigned_euk_genome`, e.g. unbinned `Tiara`/`MetaEuk`/`MarFERReT` contigs):* Added **strictly to $I^+$** as Proposition 9 relaxed pseudo-organisms (`_unbinned_P_star`, `_euk_unassigned_P_star`). They can never manufacture a resolved single-organism $d_X = 1$ call in $I^-$, while ensuring any putative `plexitrophic` call satisfiable by unbinned/unassigned community KOs is conservatively classified as `unresolved_ambig_donor`.
   - *Exact two-point call mapping (`p2_scorer.py:1500–1508`):* `d0_ambient` if $I^- = \text{d0}$; `d1_or_chain` if $I^- = \text{not\_plexi}$; `unresolved_no_donor` iff **even $I^+$ has no feasible wiring** ($\mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}(X, E) = \emptyset$, i.e. $I^+ = \text{no\_donor}$); `plexitrophic` iff **both** $I^- = \text{plexi}$ and $I^+ = \text{plexi}$ (requiring $\mathcal{W}^{(I^-)}(X, E) \neq \emptyset$ and $\mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}(X, E) \neq \emptyset$); otherwise `unresolved_ambig_donor` (including whenever $\mathcal{W}^{(I^-)}(X, E) = \emptyset$ while $\mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}(X, E) \neq \emptyset$).

## 4. Denominators, Graded Posteriors, Frozen Effect Margin $\delta$, and Gap-Free Decision Bands

### 4.1 Explicit Flow Denominators, Identification Bounds, and Graded Biological-State Posteriors
Every analysis prints an exhaustive flow table per cohort before applying any effect test:
1. Total bins in `quality_report.tsv` $\to$ consensus `dastool-*` bins $\to$ 95%-ANI dereplicated prokaryotic MAGs ($N_{\text{MAG}}$) $\to$ HQ dereplicated prokaryotic MAGs ($\ge 90\%$ completeness, $\le 5\%$ contamination: $N_{\text{HQ}}$) detected in $\ge 1$ independent event.
2. Evaluable `(HQ recipient X, endpoint/pair, event e)` tuples: separate **genome-autonomous** tuples ($X$ encodes the pathway(s) itself; $N_{\text{auto}}$) and uncalibrated/ineligible tuples from **genome-defined candidate auxotrophies** ($N_{\text{cand\_aux}}$, frozen *before* inspecting co-occurring donors or external supply $E$).
3. Under each external-supply scenario $E$, partition $N_{\text{cand\_aux}}$ exhaustively into **all five** mutually exclusive two-point states (`StratumCounts`):
   $$N_{\text{cand\_aux}} = N_{d=0,\text{ambient}(E)} + N_{d=1\text{ or chain}} + N_{\text{plexi}} + N_{\text{ambig}} + N_{\text{no\_donor}},$$
   where $U = N_{\text{ambig}} + N_{\text{no\_donor}}$ is the **unknown/unresolved support count** (`unresolved_ambig_donor` = $I^-$ and $I^+$ disagree, including $\mathcal{W}^{(I^-)}(X, E) = \emptyset \neq \mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}(X, E)$ from partial/uncalibrated eukaryotic/`unbinned` donors; `unresolved_no_donor` = $\mathcal{W}^{(I^+)}(X, E) = \emptyset$ at breadth cutoff).
4. **Propagating unknown `U` as joint Observed-minus-Null / Manski identification bounds (never coding `U` as negative, per `248923130919567360`, `248923219079643136`, `248923156647428096`):**
   - For P2-Q1 (`observed_minus_null_id_bounds`, `event_bootstrap_q1_bounds`), if observed candidate plexitrophy prevalence lies in $[a, b] = \big[N_{\text{plexi}} / N_{\text{cand\_aux}},\; (N_{\text{plexi}} + U) / N_{\text{cand\_aux}}\big]$ and the corresponding null's prevalence lies in $[c, d]$, the conservative observed-minus-null excess identification interval is $[a - d,\; b - c]$, recomputed inside the **same event-level bootstrap resamples** (`1,000` draws) to form the outer 95% confidence-identification region $[L_p^{\text{id}}, U_p^{\text{id}}]$.
   - For P2-Q2 (`manski_log_odds_bounds`), evaluate log-odds contrast $\Delta \theta_{\text{recip}}(E)$ **separately for each external-supply scenario $E$** (never pooled across $E$) between incompatible-pair (`inc`) and matched compatible-pair (`comp`) strata, computing worst-case Manski bounds ($\Delta \theta_{\text{low}}$ assigning $U_{\text{inc}} \to \text{non-plexi}$ and $U_{\text{comp}} \to \text{plexi}$; $\Delta \theta_{\text{high}}$ assigning the reverse) inside event-cluster bootstrap resamples to form $[L_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}}, U_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}}]$, reporting resolved-only sensitivity intervals alongside $U_{\text{inc}}/N_{\text{inc}}$ and $U_{\text{comp}}/N_{\text{comp}}$.
5. **Companion continuous biological-state posterior (`GradedFanInPosterior`, per `grid 248970390835478528` & `Astra 248970428366110720` / `248973173642936320`):** Alongside the primary hard two-point identification bounds, report continuous per-tuple posteriors `p_fanin_ge_2` $= P(\text{covered} \land \text{no single supplier suffices} \mid X\text{ needs all}, \text{data}, E)$ and `joint_p_plexitrophic` (`compute_pathway_posterior` + `compute_fanin_state_posterior`), marginalized over the **latent organism capability space $\Omega$** via exact inclusion–exclusion (never uniform weighting over enumerated wirings, and supporting both conditional within-donor marginals `donor_supply_probs` and arbitrary coupled within-donor joint subset PMFs `donor_joint_pmfs` such as mutual exclusion $p_{11} = 0$).

### 4.2 Data-Adequacy Gates (`check_data_adequacy_gates`, Evaluated *Before* Interpreting Any Effect Interval)
Stop and classify the test as **`Inconclusive (data-adequacy gate)`** without interpreting effect intervals if **any** of the following conditions hold:
1. Fewer than $10$ independent events contain eligible HQ candidate-auxotroph recipients (in both incompatible and compatible strata for P2-Q2).
2. `unbinned` contigs carry $> 50\%$ of detected community donor KOs for the evaluated endpoints.
3. Unresolved calls $U / N_{\text{cand\_aux}} > 0.50$ (where identification bounds dominate sampling precision).

### 4.3 Frozen Effect Margins and Gap-Free Decision Bands (Given None of Gates 1–3 Hold)
- **For P2-Q1 (`decide_p2_q1`):** $\delta_{p} = 0.05$ (+5 percentage points excess over Null 1 and Null 2b), using outer 95% confidence-identification intervals $[L_{p,1}^{\text{id}}, U_{p,1}^{\text{id}}]$ (vs. Null 1) and $[L_{p,2}^{\text{id}}, U_{p,2}^{\text{id}}]$ (vs. Null 2b), evaluated in strict precedence order: **(1) Falsified** if $U_{p,1}^{\text{id}} < 0.05$ **or** $U_{p,2}^{\text{id}} < 0.05$; **(2) Supported** if $L_{p,1}^{\text{id}} > 0.05$ **and** $L_{p,2}^{\text{id}} > 0.05$; **(3) otherwise Inconclusive** (`248924408659755008`).
- **For P2-Q2 (`decide_p2_q2`):** $\delta_{\text{OR}} = \ln 2 \approx 0.693$ ($2\times$ odds ratio increase), using outer 95% confidence-identification interval $[L_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}}, U_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}}]$ (reporting resolved-only intervals and Null 2c attribution alongside as sensitivity).

| Hypothesis | **1. Falsified (checked first after gates)** | **2. Supported** | **3. Inconclusive** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P2-Q1** (Excess over both Null 1 & Null 2b) | $U_{p,1}^{\text{id}} < 0.05$ **or** $U_{p,2}^{\text{id}} < 0.05$ *(rules out $\ge 5$ pp excess over both nulls; report $U_{p,k}^{\text{id}} < 0$ separately)* | $L_{p,1}^{\text{id}} > 0.05$ **and** $L_{p,2}^{\text{id}} > 0.05$ | Any adequacy gate holds, OR neither (1) nor (2) holds |
| **P2-Q2** (Incompatible vs. matched compatible recipient pairs) | $U_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}} < \ln 2$ *(rules out $\ge 2\times$ odds increase while still allowing a smaller positive effect, per `248921556608532480`; report $U_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}} < 0$ alongside)* | $L_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}} > \ln 2$ | Any adequacy gate holds, OR $L_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}} \le \ln 2 \le U_{\text{OR}}^{\text{id}}$ |
