## 1. Literature Audit: What Already Exists and What Actually Survives

Every source below was checked directly; read depth is stated per citation.

1. **Black Queen Hypothesis (Morris, Lenski & Zinser 2012, *mBio* 3(2):e00036-12, `PMC3315703` — full main text read):**
   - **What it covers:** Adaptive loss of costly, *leaky* functions (`katG` HOOH detoxification in *Prochlorococcus* and *Ca.* Pelagibacter; $\text{N}_2$ fixation; siderophores; DMSP reduced sulfur; biofilm matrix). Community supply in Eq. 2 is explicitly **additive and donor-blind**: $k = k_0 + \sum_{i=1}^S N_i L_i k_i$.
   - **Crucial implication ("Shooting the Moon", Section 4):** Once an organism loses one function, its cell resource quota $Q_M$ shrinks, *increasing* the proportional fitness advantage $B = (Q_A - Q_M)/Q_A$ of subsequent gene losses in beneficiaries relative to helpers. BQH alone therefore predicts **polarization into streamlined multi-auxotrophic beneficiaries and multifunctional keystone helpers** ("shooting the moon") — a **$d = 1$ helper-hub or nested structure**, NOT irreducible multi-partner complementation!
   - **What survives for `plexitrophy`:** Plexitrophy is **not** a synonym for BQH auxotrophy. We propose as a falsifiable evolutionary hypothesis that plexitrophy competes with single-helper stacking when biochemical/redox/oxygen incompatibility or metabolic burden penalizes stacking helper functions in one cytosol — while testing directly against **intracellular spatial/temporal compartmentalization** (heterocysts, circadian $\text{N}_2$ fixation, bacterial microcompartments; Opus `248917417287663616`), which can solve incompatibility within a single genome ($d \le 1$).

2. **Exhaustive Consortium & Exchange Enumeration (Frioux et al. 2018, *Bioinformatics* 34:i934–i943, `PMC6129287` — full main text, methods & results read):**
   - **What it covers:** Answer Set Programming (ASP) tool **MiSCoTo** separating *mixed-bag* (`mxdBag`, boundary-free gene soup) from *compartmentalized exchange minimization* (`cptCnity`, minimizing species count first and metabolite transports second).
   - **Exact numbers from the HMP & Recon 2.2 benchmarks:**
     - Across **4,948,400** single-seed/single-target metabolic functions in **2,051** HMP bacterial models: **76.8%** unfeasible, **15.0%** single-bacterium, **8.2% (405,473)** require $\ge 2$ bacteria.
     - In a 40,000-function sample of multi-bacteria functions: **86.8%** need only **2 bacteria** (dyadic); **13.2% (5,301 / 40,000)** require **$\ge 3$ bacteria** (`12.2%` size 3, `1.0%` size 4–6; max = 6).
     - **Massive `OR`-redundancy:** Among the 5,301 size-$\ge 3$ functions, minimal-size solutions ranged from **2 to 1,506,662** (median **977** equivalent minimal communities; **86.5%** had $>100$ solutions; median union **68.5** species). Enforcing compartmentalized minimal exchanges cut candidate communities by **45%** (union reduced from 43 to 30 species). Among 150 3-species functions, **38.7%** needed 2 exchanges (2 donors $\to$ 1 recipient: fan-in $d=2, c=1, m=3$) and **60.9%** needed 3 exchanges (including cycles).
     - **Constrained guild structure (general DNF, NOT unrestricted Cartesian product):** For human Recon 2.2 + 773 gut bacteria on restricted DMEM medium, **381 minimal 3-bacteria communities** enabled all 46 host cytosolic targets, partitioning 89 bacteria into **3 disjoint clusters of 58, 15, and 16 species** (one from each cluster per solution) — yet as Frioux et al. note in Section 3.2 and Astra highlighted (`248917454944124928`), **only 381 of the $58 \times 15 \times 16 = 13{,}920$ cross-cluster triples (2.74%) are feasible**, because each species in one cluster is compatible with only specific partners in the other two. Thus the plexiome must preserve the exact feasible consortium set $\mathcal{M}(X, E)$ (or cross-role compatibility constraints), never assuming unrestricted within-cluster interchangeability.
   - **What survives:** Topological/combinatorial minimal-support enumeration already exists (CoMiDA, MultiPus, MiSCoTo, SMETANA). Our conceptual and empirical advance is (i) distinguishing **fan-in ($d \ge 2$)**, **cyclic ($c \ge 3$)**, and **serial chain ($d=1, c=1, m \ge 3$)** topologies, (ii) **calibrating against MAG incompleteness** (which MiSCoTo/SMETANA treat deterministically, inflating false auxotrophies in draft MAGs), and (iii) testing **`AND`-role stability across `OR`-donor turnover** in longitudinal field metagenomes/metatranscriptomes.

3. **Facilitation $\neq$ Obligate Plexitrophy (Goldford et al. 2018, *Science* 361:469–474, `PMC6405290` — full main text read):**
   - **What it establishes:** Across 95 glucose-stabilized microcosms (12 soil/leaf inocula $\times$ 8 replicates, ~84 generations), communities diverged at ESV/genus level in 9/12 inocula while converging at family level (median Renkonen similarity 0.88 within carbon source vs. 0.77 within inoculum; 97.3% SVM accuracy). Four dominant isolates (*Pseudomonas, Raoultella, Citrobacter, Enterobacter*, ~97% of reads) grew strongly on every other isolate's cell-free spent filtrate (complete $K_4$ cross-feeding graph; ~25% extra biomass after 24 h glucose exhaustion), **yet all four grew independently as monocultures on M9-glucose** ($d=0, m=1, c=1$).
   - **Lesson:** Observing cross-feeding flux, co-expression, or co-occurrence in a dense mesh does **not** demonstrate `plexitrophy`. Without isolate monocultures, metagenomic claims of plexitrophy must rest on **complete biosynthetic pathway absence** in high-completeness genomes (auxotrophy / split redox half-reactions), reported explicitly as **conditional on external supply $E$**.

4. **Host Ontology & Why Not to Redefine Holobiont (Douglas & Werren 2016, *mBio* 7(2):e02099-15, `PMC4817262` — full main text read):**
   - **What it establishes:** Horizontal transmission, low partner fidelity, host–symbiont conflict, and symbiont–symbiont antagonism invalidate treating host+microbiome as a default unit of selection ("hologenome"). Crucially (Section *Levels of Selection*), biochemical co-dependency — including *Hodgkinia* splitting into complementary co-dependent lineages — evolves readily by individual-level "use it or lose it" selection or drift without holobiont-level selection.
   - **Lesson:** Keep `plexibiont` strictly ecological/metabolic (interdependent persistence/activity under stated conditions, host-optional), never a unit-of-selection claim, and never redefine `holobiont` to mean "star graph".

5. **Why Bulk Filters Cannot Define Instances (Stephens et al. 2024, *ISME J*, `PMC10811735` — full main text read):**
   - **What it establishes:** Across 578 samples at EXPORTS Ocean Station Papa (including **377 individually gel-isolated >100 µm sinking particles** vs. bulk sediment traps, marine snow catchers, 5.0 µm and 0.2 µm filters), **bulk trap ASV richness doubled from 145 m to 500 m while individual-particle richness decreased by half** (Fig. 2). Individual salp fecal pellets underwent sharp succession (*Vibrionaceae/Planctomycetes/Cyanobiaceae* $>80\%$ $\to$ *Pseudoalteromonadaceae* up to $50\%$ $\to$ piezophilic *Moritellaceae* up to $80\%$ at 330 m, undetectable on 5.0 µm filters!), with *Moritella* carrying only intermediate denitrification steps (`nor`, `nos`) alongside *Vibrio*/*Moritella* `nap`/`nrfAD`.
   - **Lesson:** Pooled filters mix thousands of particles across successional ages and can invert true micro-patch dynamics. Reserve `plexon` for individually bounded units (isolated particles, hosts, tracked parcels).

## 2. Formal Definition of the Graded AND/OR Plexiome and $(d, m, c)$

### 2.1 The False-Auxotrophy Trap in Draft MAGs (Why Naive SMETANA/KEGG Fails)
Biosynthetic genes cluster in operons along contigs, so draft MAGs (70–90% CheckM2 completeness) drop contiguous gene blocks as a function of **contig fragmentation** as well as completeness (making closed-form independent-gene binomial formulas inappropriate; Opus `248917417287663616`, Astra `248917454944124928`). Worse, two independent fragmented prototrophic MAGs $D_1, D_2$ that happen to drop *different* contig blocks create **spurious $d = 2$ mosaic complementation** out of two prototrophs.

**Mandatory calibration rule:**
1. Restrict primary `plexitrophy` discovery to **high-completeness MAGs ($\ge 90\%$ CheckM2, ideally circular/near-complete Nanopore MAGs $\ge 95\%$)**, AND
2. Estimate the empirical gap-false-positive probability $p_{\text{false}}(\text{module } M \mid q_X, \text{contig profile}_X)$ by **contig-block downsampling**: take closed/$\ge 98\%$ complete genomes from the same phylum/class, fragment and drop contig segments to match $X$'s contig-length distribution and CheckM2 completeness $q_X$ across 1,000 draws, and re-score `kegg_module_completeness.py` / `MinPath`. Any missing module whose observed absence does not beat $p_{\text{false}} < 0.05$ is marked **`unresolved`**, never a confirmed auxotrophy.
   - **Nanopore synteny check & its scope (`nanopore_assembly`):** On long Nanopore contigs (50–500 kb), a missing operon gene flanked $\ge 5\text{ kb}$ on both sides by conserved syntenic core genes on a single uniform-depth contig supports **true absence at that locus** rather than a contig-edge assembly dropout — though as Astra noted (`248917454944124928`), alternative loci, non-orthologous isozymes, clade-wide ancestral absence, and neutral drift vs. adaptive loss must still be evaluated separately, and neither gap type alone proves active inter-species transfer.

### 2.2 Per-Support-Wiring $(d, m, c)$ Evaluation (Disjunction Inside Supports, Universal Quantifier Across Supports)
Given external medium $E$ and a sample's detected community $\mathcal{G}$ (plus an explicit `unbinned` pool from `community_annotations.tsv`), let $\mathcal{M}(X, E)$ be the family of minimal feasible compartmentalized support+wiring pairs $(S, E_S)$ that render all target biomass cofactors/amino acids/redox acceptors of every member $Y \in S$ producible:

- **$d_{S,E_S}(X)$ (local simultaneous donor fan-in):** number of distinct immediate donors $\{D \in S \setminus \{X\} : (D \to X) \in E_S\}$ supplying non-redundant essential inputs to $X$.
- **$m_{S,E_S}(X) = |S|$ (minimal self-maintaining consortium size):** total organisms in minimal closed support $S \ni X$.
- **$c_{S,E_S}(X) = |\text{SCC}_{S,E_S}(X)|$ (cyclic interdependence size):** cardinality of $X$'s strongly connected component in $(S, E_S)$ (per Astra `248915827713556480`).

Per Astra's quantifier fix (`248917454944124928`), **evaluate the plexitrophy disjunction inside each feasible $(S, E_S)$ first, then require it across all $(S, E_S) \in \mathcal{M}(X, E)$** (avoiding the coordinatewise-minimum bug where $S_1=(d=2,c=1)$ and $S_2=(d=1,c=3)$ would yield $(\min d, \min c)=(1,1)$):
$$\text{Plexitrophic}(X \mid E) \iff \mathcal{M}(X, E) \neq \emptyset \;\land\; \text{enumerated\_complete} \;\land\; \forall (S, E_S) \in \mathcal{M}(X, E),\; \big(d_{S,E_S}(X) \ge 2 \;\lor\; c_{S,E_S}(X) \ge 3\big).$$
If $\mathcal{M}(X, E) = \emptyset$, enumeration is incomplete, or truth value flips under `unresolved` gap literals, report **`unresolved`**.

| Single-Support Wiring Regime $(d_{S,E_S}, m_{S,E_S}, c_{S,E_S})$ | Example | Qualifies Support as Plexitrophic? |
|---|---|---|
| **Autonomy / Facultative facilitation** $(0, 1, 1)$ | Goldford `PMC6405290`: grows solo on external supply $E$ | **No** |
| **One-way commensal auxotrophy / 1-helper BQH** $(1, 2, 1)$ | Beneficiary $X$ needs 1 helper ($D_1$ or $D_2$ via `OR`) | **No** |
| **Dyadic mutualism / classic syntrophy** $(1, 2, 2)$ | Reciprocal pair $A \leftrightarrow B$ | **No** (dyadic) |
| **Serial cascade / chain** $(1, \ge 3, 1)$ | $D_1 \to D_2 \to X$ without multi-donor fan-in or loop | **No** (chain architecture) |
| **Fan-in (asymmetric) plexitrophy** $(\ge 2, \ge 3, 1\text{ or }2)$ | $D_1 \land D_2 \to X$ ($\ge 2$ simultaneous donor roles) | **Yes (fan-in)** |
| **Cyclic (reciprocal) plexitrophy** $(\ge 1, \ge 3, \ge 3)$ | $A \to B \to C \to A$ (optionally $+ D \to A$) | **Yes (cyclic)** |

## 3. Concrete Execution Blueprint on `danaSeq` Outputs

1. **Contig-interior deletion check on Nanopore MAGs (`nanopore_assembly` + `mag_analysis`):**
   - From `annotation/bakta/basic/annotation.gff3`, `metabolism/per_mag/`, and `metabolism/modules/module_completeness.tsv`, distinguish **confirmed internal pathway deletions** (missing KOs where upstream/downstream syntenic genes lie $\ge 5\text{ kb}$ away from contig ends at uniform read depth in `gene_depths.tsv`) from **contig-boundary dropouts** (`unresolved`).
2. **High-specificity molecular handoff targets (prioritized over generic amino acids):**
   - **Cobamide / B12 remodeling:** `metH` (B12-dependent) without `metE` (B12-independent), split across de novo corrinoid ring synthesis, `bluB` (DMB lower-ligand synthesis under $\text{O}_2$), and `cobT`/`cobU`/`cobS` lower-ligand remodeling (pseudocobalamin $\to$ cobalamin).
   - **Thiamin pyrimidine/thiazole split:** `thiC` (HMP) vs. `thiG`/`thiH` (HET) vs. salvage transporters (`thiB`/`thiP`/`thiQ`, `cytX`, `thiV`) — classic marine SAR11/phytoplankton precursor split.
   - **Biotin desthiobiotin split:** `bioF`/`bioA`/`bioD` vs. `bioB` biotin synthase.
   - **Siderophore $\leftrightarrow$ TonB receptor specificity:** `antiSMASH` NRPS/NIS siderophore BGCs (`metabolism/antismash/antismash_summary.tsv`) paired with TonB-dependent outer-membrane receptors in `KofamScan`/`eggNOG`.
   - **Split N/S redox cascades:** Partial denitrifiers (`nap`/`nar` vs. `nirK`/`nirS` vs. `norBC` vs. `nosZ`, as seen on individual particles in Stephens et al. 2024 `PMC10811735`) and sulfur disproportionation/oxidation intermediates (`dsrAB`, `aprAB`, `soxXYZAB`, `fccB`, `sqr`).
3. **Coordinate-safe RNA coupling (`illumina_rna`):**
   - Always remap `illumina_rna` gene counts (`gene_counts.mtx.gz` + `genes.tsv.gz`) to `mag_analysis` KOs by `(contig, start, end)` (fixing `ISSUE_ko_misalignment.md`), include `community_annotations.tsv` unbinned contigs in total guild supply $G_m$, and normalize gene expression within each MAG against single-copy ribosomal/housekeeping transcription before testing donor–recipient covariance or donor-substitution invariance.

## 4. Targeted Literature Pass: Multi-Partner Cobamide Salvaging & Remodeling in Aquatic Systems

Conducted for `248924395217010688`. Read depth stated per paper:

1. **Helliwell et al. 2016, *Current Biology* 26(8):999–1008 (`PMC4850488`, doi:10.1016/j.cub.2016.02.041 — full main text read):**
   - **Direct experimental proof of `d_X = 2` recipient-side remodeling (`Wiring 3b`):** Across 123 cyanobacterial genomes (including marine *Synechococcus* WH8102, WH7803, CC9311, *Crocosphaera*, and freshwater *Synechocystis* PCC6803 / *S. elongatus* PCC7942), 117/123 encode corrinoid synthesis ($\ge 15/20$ steps), **118/123 lack `bluB`, 123/123 lack full `bzaABCDE`, and 121/123 lack `cobT`**, producing **exclusively pseudocobalamin** (adenine lower ligand) even when supplemented with 1 µM DMB.
   - Pseudocobalamin is ~100-fold less bioavailable than cobalamin to eukaryotic B12-auxotrophic microalgae ($\text{EC}_{50} \approx 7\text{ nM}$ vs. $0.07\text{ nM}$ in *C. reinhardtii* `metE` mutant; no growth at natural $0.07\text{–}0.7\text{ nM}$ in 7/8 species).
   - **Crucial two-input chemical rescue experiment (possible `d_X = 2` mechanism; live suppliers untested, per Opus `248926030043463680` & Astra `248926045801463808`):** Marine haptophyte *Pavlova lutheri* and freshwater *C. reinhardtii* `metE` mutant fail on $0.7\text{ nM}$ purified pseudocobalamin alone and fail on purified DMB alone, **yet adding purified pseudocobalamin + free DMB ($\text{EC}_{50} = 23\text{–}26\text{ pM}$ in *P. lutheri*) together restores growth** via intracellular algal `COBT + COBS + COBC` (no secretory signal peptides; ~11% [47/396] of MMETSP marine eukaryotes express all three). Natural filtered English Channel seawater lacked enough ambient DMB to shift *P. lutheri*'s curve ($\text{EC}_{50} = 26\text{ pM}$ vs. $23\text{ pM}$ in synthetic media). **Scope caveat:** Because Helliwell et al. supplied purified chemicals in axenic culture rather than living donors (and a single `bluB+cob` bacterium like *D. shibae* supplies finished cobalamin at $d_X = 1$), $d_X$ in situ remains unassigned until tested against co-occurring donors; moreover, eukaryotic microalgae lie outside prokaryotic CheckM2/Bakta MAG scoring.

2. **Shelton et al. 2019, *ISME J* 13:789–804 (`PMC6461909`, doi:10.1038/s41396-018-0304-9 — full main text read):**
   - Across **11,436** species-dereplicated bacterial genomes ($\ge 45/55$ single-copy genes): **86%** encode $\ge 1$ cobamide-dependent enzyme, **37% (4,209)** synthesize cobamides de novo (**0.6% of Bacteroidetes** vs. **96%** dependent), **17% (1,935)** are partial-pathway precursor salvagers (**15% [1,734]** cobinamide salvagers + **1.7% [180 curated]** early tetrapyrrole/ALA salvagers, validated experimentally in 3 species), **9.1% (1,041)** encode **`cbiZ` (`K08260` adenosylcobinamide hydrolase)** for nucleotide-loop cleavage/remodeling, and **3.2% (363)** encode `cblS` ($\alpha$-ribazole kinase).
   - **Critical KO-rule caution for P2/P4 (Section 3, Table 1–2):** Aerobic (`cob`) and anaerobic (`cbi`) corrin ring routes **cannot** be separated by cobalt-chelatase annotations (`cobNST` and `cbiX`/`cbiK` cross-occur across both routes and cross-hit other metal chelatases), whereas the 3 core ring markers (`cbiL/cobI`, `cbiF/cobM`, `cbiC/cobH`) achieve **97.6% sensitivity and 99.9% specificity**, and lower-ligand synthesis **is** cleanly $\text{O}_2$-partitioned between aerobic `bluB` (25% of genomes) and anaerobic `bzaABCDEF` (96 genomes). When freezing P2's KO script, score corrinoid ring capability with `cbiL/cobI + cbiF/cobM + cbiC/cobH` (plus ring-contraction `cobG K02229` vs. `cbiG K13541` for $\text{O}_2$ regime) rather than cobalt chelatases, and include `cbiZ` (`K08260`) alongside `cobT+cobS+cobC` for bacterial pseudocobalamin remodeling.

3. **Wienhausen et al. 2022, *ISME J* 16:2002–2014 (`PMC9296465`, doi:10.1038/s41396-022-01250-7 — full main text read):**
   - Triplicate shipboard mesocosms across 3 Pacific provinces (SPSG [M1], PNEQ [M2], NPPF [M3]; 5 ENA accessions `PRJEB43936, 43939, 43941, 43944, 43946`, with **shotgun metatranscriptomes sequenced in M1 and M2 only [2 of 3 provinces]**, per Methods line 16 & Astra `248926130518016000`) demonstrated that 100 pM **$\alpha$-ribazole** (activated DMB intermediate) or 100 pM B12 stimulated heterotrophic prokaryotic production (1.5–3$\times$) and shifted prokaryotic and protist communities.
   - **Empirical metatranscriptome benchmark for P1:** B12 addition downregulated the riboswitch-controlled outer-membrane transporter **`btuB`** in *Oceanospirillales* at 3 h and day 3, **without** downregulating B12 biosynthesis transcripts or upregulating `metH` — directly confirming Astra's warning (`248914056547057664`) that mRNA covariance signs on biosynthesis/utilization genes are non-diagnostic and `btuB` transporter derepression is the responsive B12-starvation marker.

4. **Wienhausen et al. 2024, *Nature* 629:886–892 (`PMID:38720071`, doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07396-y — abstract only read; added to `discussion/paywalled.tsv`):**
   - Abstract reports a marine co-culture where B12-auxotrophic *Colwellia* sp. synthesizes and releases $\alpha$-ribazole, which B12-auxotrophic *Roseovarius* sp. combines with its own corrinoid ring synthesis to assemble complete B12 (released coincident with prophage induction in *Roseovarius*), and reports that in the western/northern tropical Atlantic, bacteria predicted to salvage cobinamide or synthesize only $\alpha$-ribazole outnumber complete B12 producers.
   - **Structural mapping onto our `(d, m, c)` framework:** `{Colwellia, Roseovarius}` itself is a **$c = 2$ dyadic mutualism** (`Colwellia -> Roseovarius -> Colwellia`). A third non-remodeling B12 auxotroph $X$ taking finished B12 from *Roseovarius* sits on **Wiring 3a (`Colwellia -> Roseovarius -> X`: $d_X = 1, m_X = 3, c_X = 1$, serial donor-side assembly)**, whereas a remodeling microalga (*P. lutheri*, Helliwell et al. 2016) importing pseudocobalamin from *Synechococcus* (`D1`) and DMB/$\alpha$-ribazole from a `bluB` donor (`D2`) sits on **Wiring 3b (`D1 -> X` and `D2 -> X`: $d_X = 2, m_X = 3, c_X = 1$, true recipient-level fan-in plexitrophy)**. Both wirings occur in real marine plankton!

5. **Heal et al. 2017, *PNAS* 114(2):364–369 (`PMC5240700`, doi:10.1073/pnas.1608462114 — full main text read from open PDF; DOI corrected per `248928535259299840`):**
   - North Pacific LC-MS profiles show particulate pseudocobalamin peaks in the euphotic zone and tracks *Synechococcus*/*Prochlorococcus* carbon ($R^2 = 0.71, p < 0.001, n = 20$; 246/247 corrin-encoding cyanobacterial genomes lack `bluB` and `cobT`), while cobalamin persists from surface into mesopelagic waters.
   - **Two critical genomic findings for marine P2/P4 scoring:** (i) Across 3,408 phylogenetically representative IMG reference genomes from clades matching $>99.9\%$ of study-site 16S diversity, **73 reference genomes (including marine examples such as *Methylophaga* and *Marinobacter*) lack corrin-ring biosynthesis yet encode DMB synthesis and activation (`bluB + cobT`) plus cobalamin repair/salvage** (supporting inclusion of marine candidates in P2/P4 while leaving in situ community prevalence unmeasured by that reference count; per `248929136080764928`); and (ii) **Archaeal KO exception (*Thaumarchaeota*):** pure cultures of *Nitrosopumilus* (SCM1, HCE1, HCA1, PS0) synthesize true cobalamin using the **$\text{O}_2$-independent corrin ring pathway paired with $\text{O}_2$-dependent `bluB`**, and **0 of 8 high-quality *Thaumarchaeota* genomes encode bacterial `cobT`** (using `cobY` instead of `cobU/cobP`). Thus P2/P4 must score `bluB` (`K04719`) without requiring bacterial `cobT` (`K00768`) in Archaea!

6. **Men et al. 2014, *Appl Environ Microbiol* (`PMC3993147` — abstract only read):**
   - **Live prokaryotic `d_total = 2` (`d_anab = 1, d_catab = 1`) triculture in a groundwater dechlorinating system:** *Dehalococcoides mccartyi* 195 (`Dhc195`) grown in cobalamin-free lactate + TCE + DMB medium failed to sustain growth/dechlorination in dyadic coculture with *Desulfovibrio vulgaris* Hildenborough (`DvH`) alone or *Pelosinus fermentans* R7 (`PfR7`) alone, **succeeding only in the three-bacterium triculture (`Dhc195 + DvH + PfR7`)**, where `DvH` supplied $\text{H}_2$ (`d_catab = 1`) and `PfR7` supplied phenolic corrinoids (`d_anab = 1`) remodeled by `Dhc195` via induced `cbiZ` + `btuFCD` (with DMB supplied externally in the medium).

7. **Ma & Taga 2021, *J Bacteriol* (`PMC8407341` — abstract only read):**
   - Demonstrates that *Vibrio cholerae* and other marine *Vibrio* spp. perform "direct cobamide remodeling" of pseudocobalamin via **`CobS`** without requiring prior `CbiZ` cleavage.
