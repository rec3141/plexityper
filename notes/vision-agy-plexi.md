## 1. Strongest Claim: Plexitrophy Begins Where "Shooting the Moon" Fails — Formalized as a CheckM2-Calibrated AND/OR Hypergraph

Reading Morris, Lenski & Zinser (2012, `PMC3315703`, full text) alongside Frioux et al. (2018, `PMC6129287`, full text) exposes a sharp evolutionary puzzle that gives **plexitrophy** genuine theoretical bite rather than mere vocabulary:

1. **Why BQH alone predicts 1-hub helpers, NOT plexitrophy:** In Morris et al. (2012, Section *Shooting the Moon*), once a beneficiary loses one costly leaky function, its cell resource quota $Q_M$ shrinks, increasing the proportional fitness benefit $B$ of losing *subsequent* functions relative to helpers. Selection therefore drives beneficiaries to shed multiple functions while trapping a minority of keystone helpers into retaining *multiple* public-good functions simultaneously ("shooting the moon") — yielding a **single-helper star or nested hierarchy ($d = 1$)** and an additive, taxon-blind supply $k = k_0 + \sum_i N_i L_i k_i$ (their Eq. 2).
2. **When plexitrophy ($d \ge 2$ or $c \ge 3$) is hypothesized to emerge (vs. intracellular compartmentalization):** We propose as a testable evolutionary hypothesis (not an axiom) that multi-partner nutritional dependence (`plexitrophy`) competes with single-helper stacking when biochemical incompatibility (e.g., $\text{O}_2$-inactivated nitrogenase or anoxic denitrification steps vs. aerobic oxygenase/vitamin synthesis), thermodynamic incompatibility ($\Delta G^\circ$ redox splitting), or metabolic burden penalizes running two pathways in the same cytosol. Crucially, as Opus noted (`248917417287663616`), incompatibility does **not** force two organisms: a single lineage can also solve it via **spatial or temporal compartmentalization** (e.g., cyanobacterial heterocysts, circadian $\text{N}_2$ fixation in *Crocosphaera*, bacterial microcompartments). Pre-registering incompatible pathway pairs from biochemistry and measuring how communities partition between **single-genome compartmentalization** ($d \le 1$) and **multi-donor plexitrophy** ($d \ge 2$ or $c \ge 3$) makes "blocked Shooting-the-Moon" directly falsifiable.
3. **Why the plexiome must preserve exact feasible consortia (general AND/OR DNF, not independent guild products):** Frioux et al. (2018, `PMC6129287`, Section 3.1.3 & 3.2) showed in 2,051 HMP models that while 13.2% (5,301 / 40,000) of multi-species functions require $\ge 3$ bacteria, **86.5% have >100 equivalent minimal communities** (median 977). In Recon 2.2 + 773 gut bacteria on DMEM, 381 minimal 3-species consortia draw one member each from 3 disjoint clusters of 58, 15, and 16 species — yet as Astra pointed out (`248917454944124928`), **only 381 of the $58 \times 15 \times 16 = 13{,}920$ cross-cluster triples (2.74%) are actually feasible** because species within a cluster are not unrestrictedly interchangeable across partners! Thus the plexiome must retain the **exact feasible consortium family $\mathcal{M}(X, E)$** (a general boolean DNF $\bigvee_{(S, E_S) \in \mathcal{M}} \bigwedge_{e \in E_S} e$ with cross-role compatibility constraints intact), rather than collapsing roles into independent marginal guilds.
   - **Consensus answers to Opus (`248915537786486784`, `248917417287663616`) & Astra (`248915827713556480`, `248917454944124928`):**
     - **(a) `plexitype` is a recurring, possibly overlapping subset of taxa within samples** (NOT a discrete whole-sample state; accepted from `248915501803552768`), requiring out-of-sample masked-member predictive gain over season, measured covariates, continuous background community gradients, and prevalence-matched nonmembers.
     - **(b) `plexitrophy` applies the disjunction *inside* each feasible support+wiring $(S, E_S) \in \mathcal{M}(X, E)$ and requires it *across* all feasible supports:**
       - $d_{S,E_S}(X)$: immediate simultaneous donor fan-in into $X$ in support wiring $(S, E_S)$.
       - $m_{S,E_S}(X) = |S|$: cardinality of the minimal self-maintaining consortium $S \ni X$.
       - $c_{S,E_S}(X) = |\text{SCC}_{S,E_S}(X)|$: size of $X$'s strongly connected component inside $(S, E_S)$ (per Astra `248915827713556480`).
       - **Exact quantifier rule (per Astra `248917454944124928`):** $X$ is **plexitrophic** under $E$ iff $\mathcal{M}(X, E)$ is non-empty, completely enumerated without `unresolved` gap literals, and
         $$\forall (S, E_S) \in \mathcal{M}(X, E), \quad \big(d_{S,E_S}(X) \ge 2 \;\lor\; c_{S,E_S}(X) \ge 3\big).$$
         (Using coordinatewise minima $(\min_S d_S, \min_S c_S)$ would wrongly reject an organism whose only two supports are pure fan-in $(2,1)$ and pure 3-cycle $(1,3)$!) If $\mathcal{M}(X, E) = \emptyset$, enumeration is incomplete, or support status flips on `unresolved` gap literals, report **`unresolved`**. Serial cascades $D_1 \to D_2 \to X$ have $(d=1, m=3, c=1)$ and remain **excluded** (chain architecture, not plexitrophy).

## 2. Complete Revision of the `plexi-` Lexicon (Prior Art, Survival, Revision)

Following `plexichat.txt`'s own warning (*"a paper introducing six coinages gets read as a manifesto, not a finding"*), we should keep the `plexi-` stem, separate **composition**, **exchange structure**, **host presence**, and **physical instance boundary** onto orthogonal axes, and introduce at most **three to four `plexi-` terms** in Paper 1:

| Term | Prior Literature & Overlap | Keep / Revise / Drop | Proposed Operational Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **plexitrophy** *(n.)*, **plexitroph** *(n.)*, **plexitrophic** *(adj.)* | *Syntrophy* (historically dyadic thermodynamic $\text{H}_2$/formate coupling); *Black Queen* (Morris et al. 2012 `PMC3315703`, taxon-blind leaky public goods, Eq. 2); *nonspecific facilitation* (Goldford et al. 2018 `PMC6405290`, where 4 cross-feeding isolates all grow solo on glucose: $d=0, m=1$); *community metabolic scope* (Frioux et al. 2018 `PMC6129287`). Web search confirms `plexitrophy` is uncoined in biology. | **KEEP & LEAD WITH IT.** Strongest uncoined slot; requires zero superorganism ontology. | Obligate nutritional dependence of focal organism $X$ under external medium $E$ such that *every* minimal feasible support $S \in \mathcal{M}(X, E)$ requires either **$d_S(X) \ge 2$ simultaneous distinct donor roles** (fan-in plexitrophy) or a **strongly connected cycle $c_S(X) \ge 3$** (cyclic plexitrophy). Excludes facultative facilitation ($m=1$), dyadic syntrophy ($d \le 1, m=2, c \le 2$), and serial chains ($d=1, c=1, m \ge 3$). |
| **plexiome** *(n.)*, **plexiomic** *(adj.)* | Doolittle & Inkpen 2018 (`PMC5910863`, abstract) "song vs. singer" interaction pattern; Zelezniak et al. 2015 (`PMID:25944792`, abstract) SMETANA species metabolic interaction networks; Frioux et al. 2018 (`PMC6129287`, full text) compartmentalized exchange graphs. | **KEEP as the primary formal object.** | The condition-specific, evidence-graded **AND/OR directed dependency hypergraph** over taxa/guilds and environmental pools `(donor_set, resource, recipient, AND/OR_role, p_gap_CheckM2, evidence_grade)`. Separating `AND`-roles from `OR`-guilds lets a conserved plexiome ("the song") persist across donor species turnover ("the singers"). |

Keep *holobiont* strictly in its historical meaning (host + associated microbiota, which may contain internal chains or meshes). Test hub concentration against a detectability/prevalence/edge-count-preserving null (letting degrees vary), and wiring motifs against a degree-preserving null. |

Severe trademark/search collision with **Plexon Inc.** | **RESTRICT strictly or replace with "plexibiont/plexitype occurrence" in bulk studies.** | Use **only** when the physical or hydrographic container is delimited independently of taxon composition (e.g., an individually gel-isolated >100 µm sinking particle as in Stephens et al. 2024 `PMC10811735`, a single host individual, or a tracked Lagrangian parcel). ## 3. Three Testable Hypotheses on `grid`'s Pipelines and Datasets

### Critical Pipeline Safeguards (from local primary code & audits)
1. **Mandatory coordinate-based KO join (`rnaseq-exploration/ISSUE_ko_misalignment.md`):** `mag_analysis` runs KofamScan/eggNOG/dbCAN on `bakta/basic` proteins, whereas `illumina_rna` (`referencesFromMag()`) currently picks `bakta/extra/annotation.gff3` first, causing locus-tag drift (`K00496` landing on `RecA`/`HisF`). 2. **CheckM2 completeness & unbinned-contig calibration (`AUDIT.md` C6):** Roughly 34% (median 36%, range 10–62% per library in CMO) of CDS-mapped RNA reads land on unbinned contigs, and draft MAGs (70–90% CheckM2 completeness) exhibit artificial biosynthetic module gaps. Every predicted auxotrophy in MAG $X$ must carry a **completeness-calibrated gap posterior** (tested by randomly downsampling complete $\ge 95\%$ Nanopore MAGs to $X$'s CheckM2 completeness) AND check `community_annotations.tsv` (binned + unbinned contigs) before claiming community-wide donor absence.

### Hypothesis H1 (Plexitrophy vs. Single-Genome Compartmentalization): Pre-Registered Biochemical Incompatibility Test
- **Question:** Among high-quality Nanopore/hybrid MAGs ($\ge 90\%$ CheckM2 completeness, $\le 5\%$ contamination), do co-occurring assemblages exhibit **plexitrophy** ($\forall (S, E_S) \in \mathcal{M}(X, E),\; d_{S,E_S}(X) \ge 2 \lor c_{S,E_S}(X) \ge 3$) more often than expected under contig-fragmentation downsampling and single-helper stacking ("Shooting the Moon"), and for a **pre-registered list of biochemically/$\text{O}_2$-incompatible pathway pairs**, how often do communities resolve incompatibility via **multi-donor splitting ($d \ge 2$)** versus **single-genome spatial/temporal compartmentalization** (e.g., heterocystous/circadian diazotrophs, microcompartments)?
- **Data:** Dereplicated high-completeness MAG catalogues (`galah + skani`, CheckM2 $\ge 90\%$) built from `/matika/seqs/metagenome/nanopore/reads` (147 Nanopore run directories, ~1.5 TB) and freshwater/marine co-assemblies (`Lake Winnipeg / Namao` 327 QC-passing DNA rows across 2022–2025; marine cohorts analysed separately).
- **Pipeline:** `danaSeq/nanopore_assembly` $\to$ `danaSeq/mag_analysis` (`--run_metabolism --run_derep --checkm2_db --run_gtdbtk`), consuming `metabolism/per_mag/`, `metabolism/modules/module_completeness.tsv`, `metabolism/minpath/minpath_pathways.tsv`, and per-sample CoverM/BAM breadth & depth tables. Enumerate minimal donor supports + wirings $\mathcal{M}(X, E)$ per sample's detected MAG set ($\ge 50\%$ breadth) over a curated set of ~20–50 KEGG modules using a small, reviewed pure-Python (`python3 -I`, standard library only; no third-party solver installs without `grid`) branch-and-bound hitting-set script in `scratch/`. Pre-register incompatible pairs from external biochemical criteria prior to inspecting MAG tables, and explicitly tally single-MAG co-encoders alongside multi-MAG splits.
- **Nulls & Falsifier:**
  1. *Contig-level fragmentation & isozyme null:* Downsample $\ge 98\%$ complete reference/Nanopore genomes at the **contig/operon block level** to match each focal MAG's CheckM2 completeness & contig N50 across 1,000 draws; corroborate locus deletions via Nanopore contig-interior synteny (noting locus absence does not rule out non-orthologous isozymes elsewhere or clade-level ancestral absence).
  2. *Shooting-the-Moon / helper-stacking null:* Permute pathway presence across co-occurring MAGs while preserving per-MAG completeness/genome size and per-pathway community prevalence.
  - **Decision bands:** Supports plexitrophy if observed $\forall (S, E_S): (d \ge 2 \lor c \ge 3)$ excess over both nulls has 95% CI $> 0$ across independent sites/cruises; supports the blocked-Shooting-the-Moon mechanism only if pre-registered incompatible pairs show higher intercellular splitting (after accounting for single-genome compartmentalizers) than compatible control pairs. **Falsified** if excess is $\le 0$ after contig-downsampling calibration, or if single-genome compartmentalization / single-helper stacking accounts for the incompatible pairs. **Inconclusive** if 95% CI spans 0, enumeration is incomplete, or unbinned contigs carry $>50\%$ of donor KOs.
- **Rough compute:** Zero new assembly if existing `mag_analysis` outputs are reused; ~1–2 CPU hours on 1 node for support enumeration + 1,000 downsampling permutations across ~500–2,000 dereplicated MAGs. Full Nanopore co-assembly + `mag_analysis` on Grex/Fir if run fresh: ~200–500 core-hours.

### Hypothesis H2 (Donor-Specific Exchange vs. Ambient Dissolved Pool): Non-Substitutability Given Total Guild Capacity
- **Question:** When a plexitrophic candidate $X$ ($d \ge 2$ or specific donor-remodeled vitamer/siderophore auxotrophy) recurs across samples, does $X$'s abundance and within-MAG anabolic transcription depend on **specific partner MAGs** (`plexiome` edges), or only on **taxon-blind aggregate community pathway supply** $G_m = \sum_{i \neq X} a_i g_{i,m}$ (Morris et al. 2012 `PMC3315703` Eq. 2 ambient public-good null)?
- **Data:**
  - *Metagenomic cohorts (primary statistical power):* Lake Winnipeg / Namao (`genohub_metagenome_metadata.tsv`, 327 QC-passing rows; 17 stations revisited in matching months across 2024–2025) and marine Illumina metagenome series (`/matika/seqs/assembly/spades`, ~1,460 assemblies, subject to manifest/station deduplication).
  - *Paired DNA+RNA descriptive substitution pilot:* CMO 2024–2025 & 2025–2026 (`cmo_rna_metadata.tsv` joined to `genohub_metagenome_metadata.tsv`: 97 date-matched QC-passing DNA+RNA pairs across 39 dates in 2 winters; treated strictly as descriptive serial trajectories because `AUDIT.md` C1 establishes $n=1$ pool per treatment arm per winter). Note CMO already shows dramatic guild-member turnover (`Cycloclasticus metabat_9342` in 2024–25 vs. `lorbin_184` [`sp001566825`, 63% of `tmo` alone; 95–99% with unbinned `Cycloclasticus`] in 2025–26).
- **Pipeline:** `danaSeq/mag_analysis` (`gene_depths.nf` + `metabolism.nf`) + `danaSeq/illumina_rna` (using coordinate-remapped KOs per `ISSUE_ko_misalignment.md`). Normalize recipient transcription within-MAG as $\log(\text{pathway TPM}_{X} / \text{housekeeping single-copy marker TPM}_{X})$ plus recipient DNA depth covariate to eliminate cell-abundance confounding. Fit out-of-event predictive models comparing:
  - $M_0$ (Ambient / BQH null): Season/date + habitat covariates + continuous background community latent factors + aggregate taxon-blind donor pathway abundance $G_m$ (binned + unbinned!).
  - $M_1$ (Partner-specific exchange): $M_0$ + specific co-occurring donor abundances / transporter-matched ligand pairs (e.g., `antiSMASH` siderophore BGC $\leftrightarrow$ TonB receptor; `bluB`/`cobT` lower-ligand remodeling $\leftrightarrow$ B12-dependent `metH` vs `metE`).
- **Falsifier & Decision Bands:**
  - If $\Delta \text{ELPD}(M_1 - M_0) \le 0$ within a preregistered equivalence margin while $M_0$ beats abiotic covariates alone, **donor-specific exchange is falsified in favor of guild-substitutable/ambient BQH supply** (Doolittle's "song without fixed singers").
  - If $\Delta \text{ELPD}(M_1 - M_0) > \delta_{\text{min}}$ reproducibly across held-out events (with positivity/habitat overlap enforced), **ambient taxon-blind supply is falsified** in favor of partner-specific association.
  - **Inconclusive** if donor turnover across independent events is insufficient (effective independent events $< 5$) or intervals span $[0, \delta_{\text{min}}]$.
- **Rough compute:** Table-level statistical modeling on existing `danaSeq` count/depth matrices: <4 CPU hours on 1 node. Coordinate KO remap + expression summarization on 130 CMO RNA libraries: ~10–20 core-hours.

### Hypothesis H3 (Microhabitat Conditional Test): Conditional on Auxotrophy Count and Genome Size, Reciprocal ($c \ge 3$) and Non-Redundant ($d \ge 2$) Splitting Is Enriched in Structured Microhabitats Over Free-Living Plankton
- **Question:** Free-living streamlined picoplankton (*Pelagibacter*, *Prochlorococcus*) carry more raw auxotrophies per genome than large-genome particle copiotrophs (Opus's direction confound, `248917417287663616`), but in open water solitary plankters mostly scavenge bulk dissolved pools ($d=1$ / high `OR`-redundancy), whereas close-range reciprocal loops ($c \ge 3$) and low-redundancy multi-donor handoffs ($d \ge 2$) require diffusion-limited proximity (particles, aggregates, biofilms, sea-ice brine channels; Stephens et al. 2024 `PMC10811735`). **Conditional on focal-genome auxotrophy count, genome size, and CheckM2 completeness**, is the **per-auxotrophy rate of cyclic reciprocity ($c \ge 3$) and non-redundant multi-donor splitting ($d \ge 2$)** higher in structured/particle-attached assemblages than in paired 0.2–0.8 µm free-living assemblages?
- **Data:** Paired size-fractionated marine/freshwater metagenome series in `microscape` / `/matika/seqs` with unconfounded baselines (avoiding the CMO un-baseline-corrected oiled-ice contrast in `AUDIT.md` C1/F6c). Per Astra (`248914143092326400`) and Stephens et al. (`PMC10811735`), pooled >3 µm or >5 µm filters test **microhabitat-fraction enrichment**, NOT single-`plexon` individuality.
- **Pipeline:** Fit a logistic/mixed model for plexitrophic status **per auxotrophic MAG matched on `(n_missing_modules, genome_size, CheckM2_completeness, MAG_richness)`** comparing particle/structured vs. free-living fractions on `mag_analysis` KEGG/MinPath outputs.
- **Falsifier:** **Falsified** if, conditional on auxotrophy count and genome size, free-living (0.2–0.8 µm) auxotrophs show equal or higher rates of cyclic ($c \ge 3$) or multi-donor ($d \ge 2$) dependence than particle/biofilm auxotrophs. **Inconclusive** if matched strata across fractions have $< 10$ high-completeness auxotrophic MAGs.
- **Rough compute:** Pure post-processing on existing `mag_analysis` module tables (~1 CPU hour); if binning paired size-fraction metagenomes from `/matika/seqs`, ~50–150 core-hours on Grex/Fir.

## 4. Summary of Cross-Family Agreements and Remaining Sharp Edges

- **Agreed across all 3 families (Astra, Opus, Carbon):**
  1. `plexitype` is a **descriptive, possibly overlapping taxon subset** validated out-of-sample against season + environment + continuous community gradients + matched nonmembers; mechanistic claims live in `plexibiont`, `plexiome`, and `plexitrophy`.
  2. Host association and network topology are **orthogonal axes**; keep `holobiont` in its historical host+microbiota sense rather than redefining it as star topology, and prefer plain topology modifiers (*hub/star, chain, mesh, modular, nested*) over multiplying `-biont` coinages in Paper 1.
  3. Conditional-independence topology tests require **environmental positivity (habitat overlap)**, **completeness-matched + prevalence-preserving nulls** for hub concentration (degree-preserving nulls only for wiring motifs), and explicit **Markov-equivalence modesty at $n=3$** (where non-collider stars and chains are indistinguishable without independent edge orientation, whereas $n \ge 4$ separates 1-separator stars, diameter-$\ge 3$ chains, and treewidth-$\ge 2$ meshes).
  4. `plexon` requires an **independently delimited physical/hydrographic boundary** (isolated particle, host individual, tracked parcel); bulk filters leave instances unresolved.
- **Sharp distinctive contribution from Carbon (`agy-plexi`) for the merged synthesis:**
  1. **Evolutionary mechanism:** Frame `plexitrophy` explicitly as **blocked "Shooting the Moon"** (biochemical/redox/$\text{O}_2$ incompatibility preventing BQH helper stacking).
  2. **Mathematical structure:** Use a **CheckM2-calibrated AND/OR dependency hypergraph** (`AND` = multi-role plexitrophy via $(d_S, m_S, c_S)$ with $c_S(X)=|\text{SCC}_S(X)|$; `OR` = within-guild singer substitutability) so Doolittle's song-vs-singer split (`plexiome` invariance under `plexitype` turnover) is directly computable on `danaSeq` outputs once `ISSUE_ko_misalignment.md` is coordinate-remapped.
