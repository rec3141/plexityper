## 1. Cohort-Wide Common Panel & Metadata-Driven Chemistry Tiers (`NO`, `N2O`, `H2`, `N2`)

Evaluating `F_K` and `ln Q` for intermediate nitrogen-oxide steps and hydrogen-coupled steps requires activities for dissolved `NO`, `N2O`, `N2`, and `H2` (Astra `249247782548193280`; `P6` l. 180). To prevent simplex-dimension shifts across events and avoid fabricating unmeasured reactive intermediates:

1. **Common cohort panel rule:** The active `panel_tier` set is determined **once per scored cohort** from the **intersection of analytes measured across all eligible events in that cohort** (metadata-only, before any RNA counts are opened). Every event in a scored cohort is evaluated on the **exact same $R$-pathway simplex**.
2. **Core rows (`panel_tier == "core"`, 9 rows):** `AER_HET`, `DEN_NAR`, `DNRA_NRF`, `NIT_AMO`, `NIT_NXR`, `AMX_HZS`, `SRB_ORG`, `SOX_AER`, `MOX_AER`. Active whenever the P6 minimum chemistry gate (`O2`, `NO3-`, `NO2-`, `TAN` [`NH4+ + NH3`], `SO4-2`, `SigmaH2S` [`H2S + HS-`], `CH4`, `DIC`, `DOC`, `T`, `pH`) is met across the cohort.
3. **Complete $2 \times 2$ denitrification `NO2- -> N2` chemistry tiers (mutually exclusive):**
   - **Tier A (`NO` measured AND `N2O` measured -> +3 rows):** Activate `DEN_NIR` (`NO2- -> NO`), `DEN_NOR` (`2 NO -> N2O`), and `DEN_NOS` (`N2O -> N2`) (`12` rows `-H2`, `15` rows `+H2`).
   - **Tier B_N2O (`NO` unmeasured, `N2O` measured -> +2 rows):** Activate `DEN_NIRNOR` (`NO2- -> 0.5 N2O`, pooling `nirK/nirS/norBC` and cancelling unmeasured `NO`) and `DEN_NOS` (`N2O -> N2`) (`11` rows `-H2`, `14` rows `+H2`).
   - **Tier B_NO (`NO` measured, `N2O` unmeasured -> +2 rows):** Activate `DEN_NIR` (`NO2- -> NO`) and `DEN_NORNOS` (`2 NO -> N2`, `CH3COO- + 4 NO -> 2 HCO3- + 2 N2 + H+`, pooling `norBC/nosZ` and cancelling unmeasured `N2O`) (`11` rows `-H2`, `14` rows `+H2`).
   - **Tier C (`NO` unmeasured AND `N2O` unmeasured -> +1 row):** Activate `DEN_NO2N2` (`NO2- -> 0.5 N2`, pooling `nirK/nirS/norBC/nosZ` and cancelling both `NO` and `N2O`) (`10` rows `-H2`, `13` rows `+H2`). Applies automatically to `alife/mep_redox/synthetic` (`D001–D123`).
4. **Hydrogen-gated rows (`panel_tier == "h2_gated"`, 3 rows: `MCR_MET`, `HYD_FERM`, `HYD_UP`):** Active if and only if dissolved `H2` is measured across the cohort (since nanomolar `H2` shifts `4 RT ln a_{H2}` by `> 150 kJ/rxn`). Active on `alife/mep_redox/synthetic` (`13` active `Tier C + H2` panel rows).
5. **Dissolved `N2` activity:** Use measured `[N2]` when logged (`mep_redox` fixes `N2 = 500 uM`); when unmeasured on field casts, set `[N2]` to air-saturated solubility $c_{\text{N}_2,\text{eq}}(T, S)$ at $p_{\text{N}_2} = 0.78084\text{ atm}$ (Weiss 1970; `< 0.06 kJ (mol e-)^{-1}` uncertainty in `Δ_r G / n_e` for $\pm 5\%$ saturation anomalies).
6. **Sensitivity-only rows (`panel_tier == "sensitivity_only"`, 2 rows):**
   - `SOX_NO3`: MAG-resolved split reassigning `SOX_AER` markers (`sqr/fccAB/sox/sHdr`) carried on MAGs that also encode `napAB` or `narGHI` (`SNR` guild) to anoxic nitrate-coupled sulfide oxidation (`HS- + 4 NO3- -> SO4-2 + 4 NO2- + H+`).
   - `MCR_ACET`: MAG-resolved acetoclastic methanogenesis (`CH3COO- + H2O -> CH4 + HCO3-`, Jin & Kirk 2016 `PMC5112241` Table 3 Rxn 22) for `mcrABG` on *Methanothrix*/*Methanosarcina* MAGs co-carrying `cdhCDE` (`K00193, K00194, K00197`) + `acs`/`ackA-pta`.

### 2.2 Exact `cap_{r,e}` Formula (`P6` `609046bfda52` l. 73–85)
With identical CDS-to-pathway assignment $\mathcal{G}_r$ in DNA and RNA, effective CDS length $L_g = \text{end}_g - \text{start}_g + 1$ (bp), and median length-weighted depth $D_{\text{prok},e}$ across the frozen 14 universal prokaryotic single-copy markers $\mathcal{M}_{\text{prok}} = \{\text{K02886}, \text{K02906}, \text{K02874}, \text{K02946}, \text{K02961}, \text{K02967}, \text{K03043}, \text{K02470}, \text{K03553}, \text{K04043}, \text{K01872}, \text{K02355}, \text{K03046}, \text{K01889}\}$:

$$D_{\text{prok},e} = \operatorname{median}_{m \in \mathcal{M}_{\text{prok}}} \left( \frac{\sum_{g \in m} d^{\text{DNA}}_{g,e} L_g}{\sum_{g \in m} L_g} \right), \qquad \text{cap}_{r,e} = \frac{1}{D_{\text{prok},e}} \sum_{g \in \mathcal{G}_r} d^{\text{DNA}}_{g,e} \, L_g.$$

Length $L_g$ and subunit count enter once inside $\text{cap}_{r,e}$ (no separate $L_r$ factor).

### 3.2 Van 't Hoff Temperature Adjustment, Davies Activity Coefficients, and Speciation
$$\Delta_r G^\circ(T) = \frac{T}{T_r}\,\Delta_r G^\circ(T_r) + \Delta_r H^\circ(T_r)\left(1 - \frac{T}{T_r}\right), \qquad \Delta_r G_r(T, \text{pH}, a) = \Delta_r G^\circ(T) + R T \sum_s \nu_{s,r} \ln a_s.$$

- **Davies activity coefficients:** $I = 1.6 \times 10^{-5}\,\text{EC}_{25}$ ($\text{mol L}^{-1}$, from specific conductance in $\mu\text{S cm}^{-1}$) or $I = 0.0199\,S_{\text{PSU}}/(1 - 0.001005\,S_{\text{PSU}})$ ($\gamma_z = 1$ in synthetic `mep_redox`); $\log_{10} \gamma_z = -0.509\,z^2 (\sqrt{I}/(1+\sqrt{I}) - 0.3 I)$ (clipped at $I \le 0.7\text{ M}$), $a_s = \gamma_{z_s}[s]$.
- **Speciation of `DIC` (or fixed `a_CO2`), `SigmaH2S`, `TAN`:** Equilibrium constants $K_{a1,\text{C}}(T), K_{a2,\text{C}}(T), K_{a1,\text{S}}(T), K_{a,\text{N}}(T)$ are computed via $\exp(-\Delta_{\text{diss}}G^\circ(T)/(RT))$ from the **same** table above, guaranteeing exact path invariance (`< 10^{-11} kJ/mol`, Check 5) across protonation states. When total `DIC` is measured (field casts), $a_{\text{CO}_2} = \text{DIC}/(1 + K_{a1,\text{C}}/a_{\text{H}^+} + K_{a1,\text{C}}K_{a2,\text{C}}/a_{\text{H}^+}^2)$ and $a_{\text{HCO}_3^-} = a_{\text{CO}_2} K_{a1,\text{C}}/a_{\text{H}^+}$; when `DIC` is absent in `alife/mep_redox/synthetic` (`model.py:25` fixes free aqueous $\text{CO}_2$ activity at $10^{-3}$), `compute_carbonate_activities` starts directly from $a_{\text{CO}_2} = 10^{-3}$ and derives $a_{\text{HCO}_3^-} = a_{\text{CO}_2} K_{a1,\text{C}}/a_{\text{H}^+}$ (regression-checked to match an equilibrium-`DIC` fixture to `< 10^{-17}\text{ M}` across $T$ and $\text{pH}$ in `[PASS 5]`).

## 4. Kinetics $F_K$, Explicit Transfer Assumptions, $F_T$, and Turnover Basis

1. **Monod kinetics & anaerobic $\text{O}_2$ inhibition (`F_K,r`):**
   $$F_{K,r}(c) = \left(\prod_{(s, K_{S,s}) \in \mathcal{S}_r} \frac{c_s}{K_{S,s} + c_s}\right) \times \begin{cases} \dfrac{K_{I,\text{O}_2,r}}{K_{I,\text{O}_2,r} + [\text{O}_2]}, & \text{if } \texttt{o2\_inhibited} = \texttt{true}, \\[6pt] 1, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases}$$
2. **Explicitly labelled kinetic transfer assumptions & mandatory sensitivity checks (Astra `249259337457254400`, `249259926295592960`):**
   - **NO-forming nitrite reductase $K_S(\text{NO}_2^-) = 1.6\ \mu\text{M}$ (`DEN_NIR`, `DEN_NIRNOR`, `DEN_NO2N2`):** Measured directly by steady-state Michaelis–Menten kinetics on full-length cytochrome-tethered NO-forming **CuNiR (`NirK`, `[EC:1.7.2.1]`)** from *Ralstonia pickettii* (`WT RpNiR`, $K_m = 1.6 \pm 0.2\ \mu\text{M}$; canonical 2-domain *Alcaligenes xylosoxidans* `WT AxNiR` $K_m = 21 \pm 2\ \mu\text{M}$; **Hedison et al. 2019** *ACS Catal.* 9:6087–6099, `PMC7007197` Table 4). Applying $1.6\ \mu\text{M}$ to pooled `nirK + nirS` (`cd1NiR` `NirS`, `K15864`) is an explicit **isozyme transfer assumption**, tested via the pre-registered $0.5\times / 2\times$ $K_S$ sensitivity (`P6` l. 211) plus a `21 uM` 2-domain `NirK`/`NirS` check. By contrast, **Silveira et al. 2010** (`PMC2905729` Table 1, ammonia-forming `ccNiR`/`NrfA` `[EC:1.7.2.2]`, $K_M^{\text{app}} = 6.4\ \mu\text{M}$ with diquat, range `2.7–15.0 uM` across mediators) is used strictly for `DNRA_NRF`.
   - **Process-to-step denitrification $K_{I,\text{O}_2}$ transfer (`DEN_NAR` `3.0 uM`, `DEN_NIR`/`DEN_NOR`/`DEN_NIRNOR` `0.297 uM`, `DEN_NORNOS`/`DEN_NO2N2` `0.255 uM`, `DEN_NOS` `0.205 uM`):** **Dalsgaard et al. 2014** (`PMC4217175` Table 3) measured community OMZ $\text{O}_2$ half-inhibition ($C_{50}$) for net $\text{N}_2\text{O}$ production (`297 ± 139 nM`), net $\text{N}_2$ production (`205 ± 34 nM`), and total $\text{N}_2 + \text{N}_2\text{O}$ production (`255 ± 88 nM`), and **Kalvelage et al. 2011** (in **Canfield & Kraft 2022** `PMC9828761` p. 5337) measured community $\text{NO}_3^- \to \text{NO}_2^-$ half-inhibition (`3000 nM`). Assigning these community process $C_{50}$ values to individual enzymatic steps is an explicit **model-transfer assumption**, tested by a mandatory **uniform-`K_{I,O2}` sensitivity run** setting $K_{I,\text{O}_2} = 0.255\ \mu\text{M}$ across all active denitrification steps.
3. **Per-electron $F_{T,r}$ and specific electron-turnover basis (`kcat_e_rel = 1.0`):**
   $$F_{T,r} = \max\!\left(0,\; 1 - \exp\!\left(\frac{\Delta_r G_r / n_{e,r} + \Delta G_{\min,e,r}}{\chi_{e,r} R T}\right)\right)$$
   with $\chi_{e,r} = \chi_{\text{rxn},r}/n_{e,r}$ and $\Delta G_{\min,e,r} = (m_{P,r}/n_{e,r})\Delta G_P$ ($\Delta G_P = 45\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$, Jin & Kirk 2016 `PMC5112241` Eq. 10–11 & Table 3; $\Delta G_{\min,e,r} = 0$ LaRowe 2012 sensitivity per Coon, ..., LaRowe et al. 2024 `PMC11594503`). Catalyst power $P_r = k_{\text{cat},e,r} F_{K,r} F_{T,r} (-\Delta_r G_r / n_{e,r})$ uses the uniform electron-turnover basis $k_{e,0} = 8.0 \times 10^{-6}\text{ mol } e^-\text{ g}^{-1}\text{ s}^{-1}$ (`kcat_e_rel = 1.0`, Jin & Kirk 2016 Table 3; Vallino & Huber 2018 universal $\nu^*$), cancelling in $\pi_{r,e}$ across all models.

## 5. Frozen Likelihood, Read-Assignment, Single Primary Block Weighting, and Check 7 Verification

1. **Panel-conditioned DM likelihood & common floor (`P6` l. 188–194):** Score $\mathbf{n}_{\text{panel},e} \mid N_{\text{panel},e} \sim \text{DM}(N_{\text{panel},e}, \phi^{-1}\boldsymbol{\pi}_e^{(M)})$ on events with $N_{\text{panel},e} \ge 100$ panel RNA reads, mixing every model's normalized weights $\tilde{\boldsymbol{\pi}}_e^{(M)}$ (falling back to uniform over $\text{cap}_{r,e} > 0$, or $1/R$ if all $\text{cap}_{r,e} = 0$, when $\sum_r w_{r,e}^{(M)} = 0$) with the frozen observation floor $\varepsilon_{\text{obs}} = 10^{-4}$: $\pi_{r,e}^{(M)} = (1 - \varepsilon_{\text{obs}})\tilde{\pi}_{r,e}^{(M)} + \varepsilon_{\text{obs}}/R$ (Check 6 PASS; sensitivity $\varepsilon_{\text{obs}} \in \{10^{-5}, 10^{-3}\}$ and outside-panel `"other"` common-mass check).
2. **Read assignment:** Exclude SAM secondary (`0x100`) and supplementary (`0x800`) alignments (`MAPQ >= 20` sensitivity); assign multi-CDS-overlapping reads by strictly greater bp overlap (drop exact ties); apply identically to DNA and RNA.
3. **Single primary block weighting & cohort block gate (`P6` l. 195–199; Astra `249259337457254400`):**
   - **Independent block $c(e)$:** `cast_id` (water-column profiles) or `mesocosm_id` (mesocosm time series).
   - **Block eligibility gate:** Require $C \ge 6$ independent blocks ($\ge 3$ blocks containing oxic events $[\text{O}_2] \ge 10\ \mu\text{M}$ and $\ge 3$ blocks containing suboxic/anoxic events $[\text{O}_2] < 10\ \mu\text{M}$), $\ge 20$ total events, and $\ge 4$ active panel pathways with $n_{r,e} > 0$ in $\ge 50\%$ of events.
   - **Primary score (`equal-block` weighting):**
     $$S_M = \frac{1}{C} \sum_{c=1}^{C} \frac{1}{m_c} \sum_{e \in c} \log p_{\text{DM}}\!\left(\mathbf{n}_{\text{panel},e} \;\middle|\; \boldsymbol{\pi}_e^{(M)}, \hat{\phi}_{\text{train}}\right),$$
     where $m_c$ is the number of eligible events in block $c$. Equal-event weighting ($1 / \sum_c m_c$) is secondary sensitivity only.
4. **End-to-end synthetic scoring smoke test (`Check 7` in `scratch/build_and_verify_p6_table.py`, Astra `249259926295592960`, `249262820092723200`, `249263953729208320`):**
   - **Denominators & `unresolved_oxidase_donor` isolation:** `Tier C + H2` activates **`13` panel rows**; `alife/mep_redox` simulates **`12` populatable rows** (`DNRA_NRF` is unmodeled in `mep_redox/model.py`). Due to simulated `60–100%` per-MAG contig loss (`observe.py` l. 77), `D001` assembles **`11/13` panel rows** (`11/12` simulator-populatable; `69` primary catabolic CDSs, `9` `unresolved_oxidase_donor` CDSs, `631` prokaryotic housekeeping CDSs) and `D002` assembles **`11/13` panel rows** (`11/12` simulator-populatable; `77` primary catabolic CDSs, `6` `unresolved_oxidase_donor` CDSs, `691` housekeeping CDSs), while the union **`D001 ∪ D002` exercises `12/13` panel rows (`12/12` simulator-populatable)**.
